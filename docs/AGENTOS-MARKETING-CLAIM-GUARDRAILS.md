# AgentOS Marketing Claim Guardrails

## Purpose
Keep marketing creative aligned with what AgentOS can demonstrate, while allowing clearly labelled concepts and future-product positioning.

## Approved positioning
- AgentOS: one platform coordinating multiple AI models and specialist agents.
- Working campaign line: **One platform. Every AI.**
- Supporting line: **Make the most of AI.**
- AI Team: Willow (Planner), Isla (Executor), Jack (Guardian / Green Agent), Henry (Assurer / PRS).
- Workflow story: Understand → Plan → Execute → Challenge → Assure → Deliver.
- Three interface modes: Simple, Essentials, Tech Head.
- Chat is a first-class AgentOS control surface.

## Avoid without evidence
Do not claim specific customer outcomes, savings, accuracy, uptime, security certification, compliance, autonomy, model superiority, or production integrations unless independently supported by current evidence.

Do not use generated mockups as proof that a feature exists in production.

## AI-provider references
When showing third-party AI/model names or logos in marketing concepts, treat them as ecosystem examples only until trademark/logo permissions and current integration support are verified. Do not imply endorsement or partnership.

## Character/IP guardrail
The canonical characters must remain original AgentOS characters. Avoid visual or verbal references that could create confusion with established entertainment franchises or other AI brands. Do not use Tolkien-derived campaign wording such as “One AI to rule them all” as the preferred commercial line; use “One platform. Every AI.” instead.

## Creative status labels
Marketing assets should be internally classified as one of:
- CONCEPT — exploratory visual or message.
- DEMONSTRABLE — supported by a reproducible product demonstration.
- VALIDATED — supported by customer/user evidence under the commercial evidence gates.
- PRODUCTION — actually implemented and verified in the relevant AgentOS release.

## Review rule
Any asset moving from concept toward public advertising should pass brand/IP review and evidence review before publication.
