# AgentOS AI Team Brand System

**Status:** Marketing strategy / creative direction
**Owner:** Marketing Overseer
**Updated:** 2026-09-02

## 1. Brand idea

**One AI to rule them all.**

AgentOS makes the most of AI by coordinating the right free and paid AI models, specialist agents and governed workflows for the job.

The customer does not need to choose one AI for everything. AgentOS is the coordination layer that helps them use the right intelligence at the right time while built-in controls manage security, quality, cost and speed.

## 2. The AI Team

| Character | Role | Human cue | Core line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Willow | Planner | Calm, thoughtful, analytical | “I’ve worked out the best approach.” |
| Isla | Executor | Energetic, practical, decisive | “I’ll get it done.” |
| Jack | Guardian / Green Agent | Curious, sceptical, protective | “Wait. Let’s check that.” |
| Henry | Assurer / PRS | Precise, methodical, trustworthy | “I’ve independently verified it.” |

### Team interaction

**Willow → Isla → Jack → Henry → AgentOS**

1. **Understand** — Willow gathers context and understands the goal.
2. **Plan** — Willow maps the best approach.
3. **Execute** — Isla performs authorised work.
4. **Challenge** — Jack checks for problems, weaknesses and improvements.
5. **Assure** — Henry independently verifies important results.
6. **Deliver** — AgentOS releases the governed result when the required gates are satisfied.

## 3. AgentOS relationship

The characters are not four competing assistants. They are specialist roles coordinated by AgentOS.

- **AgentOS** = operating system / control plane.
- **Willow** = planning intelligence.
- **Isla** = execution coordination.
- **Jack** = independent challenge and quality/security improvement role.
- **Henry** = independent assurance / verification role.
- **Overseer** = governance and portfolio/project supervision above individual workflows.

## 4. Audience strategy

The AI Team is universal. Character usage can adapt to context without creating separate brands.

Primary audience families:
- Personal
- Home & Family
- Students
- Developers
- Creators
- Freelancers
- Professionals
- Small businesses
- Teams
- Larger organisations

The same characters explain different workflows. Marketing should change the **job being done**, not the underlying identity of the team.

## 5. Visual direction

- Premium modern technology-product aesthetic.
- Deep navy / charcoal foundation.
- Restrained electric-violet, teal and green accents.
- Strong typography and generous spacing.
- Characters should feel capable, contemporary and approachable.
- Consistent proportions, wardrobe language and rendering style across all four characters.
- Role-specific props or visual cues may reinforce personality.
- Product UI and system diagrams should remain clean enough to work without the characters.

### Character cues

**Willow**
- Calm posture and attentive expression.
- Notebook/tablet/research cues.
- Green-led visual identity.

**Isla**
- Forward-moving posture and confident expression.
- Action/device/coordination cues.
- Violet/pink-led accent identity.

**Jack**
- Slightly inquisitive stance.
- Magnifier/check/inspection cues.
- Green-led guardian identity.

**Henry**
- Precise, composed stance.
- Verification/document/evidence cues.
- Blue/teal-led assurance identity.

## 8. Proof and claims rules

Creative can show intended workflows and product concepts, but must not imply customer validation where none exists.

Do not claim:
- Fully autonomous production operation without evidence.
- Replacement of systems of record.
- Guaranteed savings or outcomes.
- Customer adoption, satisfaction or willingness to pay without evidence.

Prefer claims that can be demonstrated:
- Right AI for the task.
- Governed execution.
- Human control.
- Verification and assurance.
- Model/provider flexibility.
- Cost-aware routing.
- Cross-system workflow coordination.

## 9. Creative guardrail

The characters should make AgentOS easier to understand, not make it look like a toy.

The visual system must communicate **trust + capability + humanity** before personality.
