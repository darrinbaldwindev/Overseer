## Executive conclusion

The public evidence identifies **Global Shop Group / GlobalShopCo as a commerce-and-fashion company**, but it does not establish a currently purchasable, exact GlobalShopCo-owned product. The official corporate site says only that the company partners with and cultivates companies at the intersection of digital commerce and fashion.[1] A separate storefront using the `globalshopco.com` domain exposes broad category navigation—fashion, beauty, and home—but its public all-products collection returned **“No products found”** on the observation date.[2] Therefore, exact product names, SKUs, displayed prices, stock, delivery promises, wholesale terms, marketplace permissions, Prime status, and current availability are **UNKNOWN**, not negative findings.

Public evidence does support a product/service scope for the associated ANKA platform. ANKA describes seller-facing Shop, Pay, and Ship services, and gives examples of African-made physical goods such as fashion, jewelry, fabrics, and beauty products.[3] A contemporaneous trade report states that Global Shop Group acquired ANKA in October 2025 and that ANKA's marketplace originally covered African-inspired fashion products before expanding into payments, logistics, digital content, and training.[4] This is evidence of an associated commerce platform and product categories, not proof that any particular item is owned, stocked, or sold directly by GlobalShopCo.

## Product and price evidence

The only directly observed storefront evidence is the public collection page. It supports the existence of category navigation and a USD country/region selector, but it returned no product records. There is consequently no defensible calculation for unit price, landed cost, gross margin, shipping cost, or sell-through. Any such calculation would require an exact product listing and verified fulfillment inputs.

A useful evidence rule for this lane is:

> **Category presence is not product evidence.** A category label can establish that a site presents a merchandising taxonomy. It cannot establish that a named item exists, is in stock, is sold by the target entity, can be resold, or has a stable price.

## Findings by evidence class

### VERIFIED FACT

1. The official corporate site publicly describes Global Shop Group's focus as digital commerce and fashion.[1]
2. The public `globalshopco.com` collection page presented broad apparel, beauty, and home categories but returned “No products found” when observed on 2026-09-18.[2]
3. ANKA's official site publicly describes Shop, Pay, and Ship services and gives examples of African-made physical products.[3]
4. Ecofin Agency reported the October 2025 acquisition of ANKA by Global Shop Group and described ANKA's historical marketplace, payment, and logistics scope.[4]

### REASONABLE INFERENCE / ESTIMATE

1. GlobalShopCo may be an operating or holding group whose public website is not itself a retail catalog, because the corporate statement emphasizes partnering with and cultivating companies rather than listing goods.[1]
2. The `globalshopco.com` storefront may be a separate, incomplete, unpublished, empty, or temporarily unavailable retail implementation. This is an inference from the domain and page structure, not a verified relationship to the corporate site.[1][2]
3. ANKA is plausibly the principal publicly documented commerce platform associated with Global Shop Group after the reported acquisition. The relationship is supported by the cited report, but the current legal and operational structure is not independently verified here.[3][4]
