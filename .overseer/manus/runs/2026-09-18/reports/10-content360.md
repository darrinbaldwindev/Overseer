## Scope and identity

The name **Content360** resolves to at least two distinct public lineages. First, `content360.io` / `get.content360.io` market a web-based social-content automation product associated with a founder named Dom (the landing page identifies him as “Dom Ricci”). Second, Marketing-Interactive operates a separate **Content360** conference and awards program for Asian content marketers. They should not be merged as one company, product, or evidence base without further identity verification.

## Reasonable inferences / estimates

1. **REASONABLE INFERENCE:** The software landing pages are conversion-oriented direct-response marketing rather than neutral product documentation: they use urgency (“last batch”), social proof, a founder letter, a guarantee, and comparative value-stack pricing. This increases the need for independent verification before relying on numerical or availability claims.
2. **REASONABLE INFERENCE:** The two product pages may represent different campaign versions or time periods, because they disagree on customer count, annual price, platform count wording, and future-price framing. A dated snapshot or version history would be needed to reconcile them.
3. **REASONABLE ESTIMATE:** If the $67 price and $47/month comparison were both genuine and service access persisted, nominal break-even would occur during month two. This is a sensitivity illustration, not an ROI or earnings claim.
4. **REASONABLE INFERENCE:** The Marketing-Interactive conference is a potentially relevant awareness, partnership, and content-marketing channel for the broader marketing ecosystem, but the official page does not establish any relationship with the `content360.io` software.
