## Meaning of CORE / APPROVED

`CORE` means strategically approved for AgentOS inclusion, integration planning, capability modelling, provider-neutral routing consideration, documentation and FAQ coverage.

It does **not** by itself mean:

- credentials are available;
- a live connection is configured;
- every capability is healthy or supported;
- every capability is production-ready;
- a subscription is active;
- a paid plan is required or approved;
- AgentOS has execution authority;
- AgentOS may spend money;
- AgentOS may bypass consent, policy, budgets, Green or PRS;
- production autonomy is enabled;
- provider output is independently verified;
- unrestricted access is authorised.

Entitlement, connection, capability, health, authority, consent, budget, execution, verification and assurance remain separate states.

## FAQ presentation levels

The core FAQ system should support three depths while sharing one canonical truth model:

### Everyday
Plain-language answers: what it is, why use it, cost, connection, permissions, safety, privacy and common problems.

### Advanced
Accounts, scopes, budgets, scheduling, provider switching, recovery, data movement, health and governance.

### Tech Head
OAuth/API/MCP boundaries, capability contracts, authority provenance, correlation, idempotency/replay semantics, evidence/receipt behaviour, version compatibility, Green/PRS boundaries and technical limitations.

Simple / Essentials / Tech Head are presentation views only. They must not silently alter authority, entitlement or capability.

## Governance

Core status is an inclusion/documentation/product decision. It never overrides AgentOS authority, consent, capability, policy, risk, budget, approval, execution, evidence, Green or PRS controls.

No provider may become a second AgentOS control plane. External capability provides capability, never authority.

AgentOS remains provider/model/tool agnostic and free-first. Commercial relationships, affiliate economics or referral payments must never alter technical worker ranking or governance decisions.
