## Current AgentOS marketing thesis

Do **not** differentiate AgentOS primarily on generic AI chat, multi-model support, MCP, PowerShell, computer control, scheduling or local files. These are increasingly commoditised.

The stronger positioning is **governed AI execution**:

- user remains in control;
- bounded permissions/capabilities;
- explicit authority;
- evidence of what happened;
- durable mission state;
- recovery rather than silent failure;
- replay/duplicate protection;
- cost/capability controls;
- Green verification;
- PRS independent adversarial assurance;
- provider/model neutrality;
- useful coordination of free and paid AI.

Working beta line: **Give AgentOS a real job.**  
Supporting idea: **Stay in control. See what it did.**  
Broader brand direction retained: **One AI to rule them all.**

Internal north-star: AgentOS could become the trusted AI control layer on every computer. It should **not** try to replace Windows or directly implement every low-level PC action. Preferred architecture: AgentOS owns intent/orchestration/authority/evidence/recovery/assurance; a small trusted native worker covers essential local operations; specialist plugins/connectors/workers handle specialised execution.
