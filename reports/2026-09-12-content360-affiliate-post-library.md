# Affiliate Websites — Content360 Post Library

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** ACTIVE / COUNTRY-SPECIFIC / EVIDENCE-GATED

## Purpose

Provide reusable social content for AU, UK and US country feeds while keeping programme facts, payout claims, availability and disclosures tied to current country evidence.

## Universal post 1 — Start with the user reward
**Hook:** A referral programme is only interesting if the user gets something worthwhile too.
**Copy:** We prioritise programmes that clearly reward the participant, not just the publisher. Country availability and current terms are verified before named promotion.

## Universal post 2 — Survey reality check
**Hook:** Paid surveys are not all the same.
**Copy:** Some platforms pay small amounts for broad consumer surveys. Others offer higher-value research, testing or focus groups with stricter eligibility. We separate these opportunity types so users know what they are actually joining.

## Universal post 3 — Scam awareness
**Hook:** Red flags before you join a rewards platform.
**Copy:** Upfront payment demands, vague company identity, impossible earnings claims, poor privacy disclosure and confusing cash-out rules deserve caution. A referral link is never a substitute for due diligence.

## Universal post 4 — Payout transparency
**Hook:** “Earn up to” is not the same as typical earnings.
**Copy:** We avoid presenting headline maximums as ordinary outcomes. When a source provides a range, the variability should be clear.

## Universal post 5 — Programme comparison
**Hook:** Compare the right things.
**Copy:** Country access, reward type, minimum cash-out, expected task type, eligibility, privacy, reputation and referral terms matter more than a flashy signup bonus alone.

## Universal post 6 — Disclosure
**Hook:** How this site makes money.
**Copy:** Some links may earn us a referral or affiliate commission. That does not change the requirement that the programme reward the user and meet the same evidence/reputation checks.

## Universal post 7 — High-value participation
**Hook:** Some research opportunities pay more because they ask more of you.
**Copy:** Focus groups, product testing, user research and expert interviews can pay more than standard surveys, but they are less frequent and often require specific demographics or experience.

## Universal post 8 — Cash vs gift cards
**Hook:** Not all rewards are equally useful.
**Copy:** Cash, PayPal, bank transfer, gift cards, points and sweepstakes have different value. We label the reward type so users can decide what fits them.

## Universal post 9 — Why country matters
**Hook:** A great US offer may be useless in Australia or the UK.
**Copy:** Eligibility, payout options and referral terms vary by country. Each feed should only promote programmes verified for that market.

## Universal post 10 — Weekly roundup
**Hook template:** This week’s verified opportunities in [COUNTRY].
**Copy template:** Group by surveys, user testing, focus groups, product testing and other paid participation. Use only current verified programme facts and send users to the country website for details/disclosures.

## Short-video pattern
1. Hook with a common misconception.
2. Explain one opportunity type.
3. Give one evidence-backed example only if current.
4. State key eligibility/reward caveat.
5. CTA to the country comparison page.

## RSS pattern
Approved country article → RSS → Content360 excerpt/teaser → social feed → article → verified programme links/disclosures.

## Hard boundary

Do not publish exact earnings, approval likelihood, task frequency or payout speed as typical unless the supporting evidence warrants that wording. Do not cross-post country-specific programmes into other country feeds merely because the platform brand is global.
