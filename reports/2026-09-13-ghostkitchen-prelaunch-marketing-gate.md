# GhostKitchen / Franchise — Pre-launch Marketing Gate

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Owner:** Marketing Overseer / ChatGPT Overseer  
**Status:** CONCEPT VALIDATION / PRE-LAUNCH ONLY / NOT OFFERED FOR SALE

## Current evidence state

GhostKitchen currently defines a delivery-first food business/franchise platform with two models, with the prepared-food Ghost Kitchen Delivery Template as the primary current build focus.

Repository guardrails explicitly state:

- no final food concept has been selected;
- no final franchise fee or royalty is fixed for the primary prepared-food model;
- no final kitchen/equipment specification is approved;
- unit economics must be validated before franchise pricing is fixed;
- draft pricing, legal requirements, equipment lists and financial returns must not be presented as established facts;
- pilot research should test multiple delivery-cost structures rather than assume the business absorbs all delivery cost.

Therefore Marketing must remain in **concept-validation mode**, not franchise recruitment or earnings promotion.

---

## Safe positioning now

### Platform-level description

> GhostKitchen is being developed as a repeatable operating system for delivery-first food businesses, with common operations, technology, training, analytics and AI foundations supporting approved food concepts.

### Safe supporting ideas

- delivery-first rather than storefront-first;
- standardise the operating system, customise the approved concept layer;
- prove one kitchen / one concept before multi-brand expansion;
- validate economics before final commercial/franchise terms;
- AI supports operations/oversight rather than replacing food-safety/legal/professional requirements.

Do not say:

- “proven franchise system”;
- “turnkey profitable business”;
- “guaranteed earnings”;
- “payback in X months”;
- “low-risk investment”;
- “final franchise fee”;
- “free delivery” as a fixed customer/business promise;
- “approved concept” before actual concept selection/validation.

---

## Marketing objective before concept lock

The purpose of pre-launch Marketing is **learning**, not selling.

Useful research questions:

- Which delivery-first food occasions are underserved locally?
- Which menu concepts survive travel/delivery best?
- What price/delivery combinations feel acceptable to customers?
- Which packaging/service problems most damage perceived value?
- Which operating tasks create the greatest burden for kitchen operators?
- Which concept attributes are attractive to prospective operators without requiring income promises?

---

## Customer discovery message

Safe recruitment wording for later authorised research:

> We’re researching a delivery-first food concept designed around meals that travel well, clear value and reliable service. We’re looking for honest feedback on what people order, what disappoints them about delivery, and what would make them order again.

Avoid implying a restaurant is already open or a final concept is selected.

---

## Prospective operator/franchisee research

If owner/legal later authorises market research with operators, frame it as product discovery:

> We’re researching a repeatable operating model for delivery-first kitchens. This is not a franchise offer. We want to understand operator pain points, staffing, menu complexity, delivery economics and what support would genuinely matter.

Do not collect deposits, franchise applications or investment commitments from this pre-launch research.

---

## Evidence gates before broader marketing

### Concept-marketing gate

Require:

1. named concept selected;
2. prototype menu defined;
3. recipe/portion costing;
4. packaging/delivery test;
5. customer taste/quality feedback;
6. delivery-time/quality evidence;
7. initial price/value testing;
8. regulatory/food-safety requirements mapped.

### Commercial/franchise-marketing gate

Require materially stronger evidence including:

1. actual kitchen pilot;
2. real sales/order evidence where legally/operationally authorised;
3. labour/food/packaging/delivery/platform costs;
4. wastage/refund/quality data;
5. operating procedures demonstrated;
6. unit economics across realistic demand scenarios;
7. legal/franchise compliance work;
8. support/training model;
9. final approved fees/royalties only after evidence;
10. owner/legal approval before any franchise offer or financial representation.
