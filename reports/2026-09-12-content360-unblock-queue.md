## Evidence completion rule

A worker saying an item is complete is not enough. Marketing may upgrade a blocked campaign only when the underlying project evidence is durable, specific to the claim, current, and independently verified where the project governance requires it.

## Recheck priority

Dynamic commercial facts should be rechecked immediately before scheduling if they can change quickly: price, stock, shipping, programme terms, referral status, payout thresholds and availability.

## Outcome

The unblock queue should be treated as a demand signal from marketing back into project execution. Project workstreams should not change architecture solely for marketing; they should produce the evidence needed from the canonical product/runtime systems.
