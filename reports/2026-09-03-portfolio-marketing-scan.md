## Evidence rules

Marketing claims remain classified as FACT, VERIFIED, INFERENCE, RECOMMENDATION, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN, CLAIMED or IMPLEMENTED as appropriate. Repository existence, historical agent research and mockups do not prove customer demand, product capability, commercial performance or implementation.

## Separation

Marketing actions in project repositories are documentation/strategy contracts only. They do not change runtime, scheduling, permissions, credentials, financial authority, production deployment or project architecture.
