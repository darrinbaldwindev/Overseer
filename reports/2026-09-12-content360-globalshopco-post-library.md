# GlobalShopCo — Content360 Post Library

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** ACTIVE / EVIDENCE-GATED  
**Purpose:** Provide reusable social content that can be scheduled through Content360 without inventing product facts.

## Core positioning

GlobalShopCo should feel useful, practical and selective rather than like a generic dropshipping catalogue. The social feed should teach, compare, solve small problems and explain why products are selected.

## Post 1 — Useful products, carefully selected
**Hook:** Not every product belongs in the store.

**Copy:** GlobalShopCo is being built around a simple idea: useful products, carefully selected. We look for practical items with a clear use case, reliable supply and delivery economics that make sense before they earn a place in the catalogue.

**CTA:** See what made the shortlist.

## Post 2 — Home organisation
**Hook:** Small spaces feel bigger when everything has a place.

**Copy:** Good storage is not about owning more containers. It is about reducing friction. The strongest home-organisation products solve one specific problem well: wasted vertical space, messy drawers, awkward corners, loose cables or hard-to-reach items.

**CTA:** Explore practical organisation ideas.

## Post 3 — Product selection transparency
**Hook:** Sometimes the best product decision is “not yet.”

**Copy:** If freight, supplier reliability, returns or landed-cost evidence is unclear, we do not treat a product as promotion-ready. A good-looking listing is not enough.

**CTA:** Follow the build behind GlobalShopCo.

## Post 4 — Pet utility
**Hook:** Pet products should make daily life easier, not just look clever.

**Copy:** We prioritise practical pet accessories that solve everyday jobs: storage, travel, feeding, cleanup, organisation and comfort. Product-specific recommendations are only promoted once the underlying facts are current.

## Post 5 — Baby and home safety
**Hook:** Safety content needs a higher bar.

**Copy:** For baby and home-safety products, useful marketing starts with accurate specifications and current evidence. We will not turn an unverified supplier claim into a safety promise.

## Post 6 — Free delivery economics
**Hook:** “Free delivery” is only useful if the numbers work.

**Copy:** GlobalShopCo is designed around a free-delivery customer proposition, so freight has to be considered before a product is treated as commercially viable. That is why freight evidence matters as much as the product price.

## Post 7 — Problem/solution video
**Script:** Start with one annoying everyday problem. Show the clutter, inconvenience or wasted space. Introduce one evidence-cleared product or category solution. End with: “Simple products. Useful jobs. GlobalShopCo.”

## Post 8 — Comparison guide
**Hook:** The cheapest option is not always the best buy.

**Copy:** Compare products on the things that matter for the job: dimensions, materials, durability, delivery, returns, compatibility and total value. Price is one input, not the whole answer.

## Post 9 — Behind the shortlist
**Hook:** What gets a product rejected?

**Copy:** Common reasons include unclear freight, weak supplier evidence, unrealistic margins, poor returns terms, questionable product claims or no clear customer problem solved.

## Post 10 — Kitchen and pantry
**Hook:** Pantry organisation works best when it removes a repeated annoyance.

**Copy:** Better visibility, easier access and sensible use of shelf depth matter more than decorative storage alone. We look for products that improve how a space actually works.

## Post 11 — Seasonal utility
**Hook:** Seasonal products should still earn their place.

**Copy:** A seasonal trend can create demand, but we still apply the same product test: real use case, current supply, viable delivery, sensible returns and evidence-backed claims.

## Post 12 — Trust post
**Hook:** Product facts can change.

**Copy:** Prices, stock, freight and supplier terms move. Promotion should stop when the evidence is stale. GlobalShopCo content is designed to be refreshed rather than left online indefinitely as if nothing changes.

## Post 13 — Customer-question format
**Hook:** What household job annoys you most?

**Copy:** Cable clutter? Pantry chaos? Pet cleanup? Car organisation? Small-space storage? Tell us the problem first. We will research the product second.

## Post 14 — Category roundup
**Hook:** This week’s useful-product themes.

**Copy template:** Home organisation • Pet utility • Kitchen storage • Cleaning tools • Everyday safety. Exact products rotate only when current evidence supports them.

## Post 15 — Store philosophy
**Hook:** A smaller useful range beats a huge noisy catalogue.

**Copy:** GlobalShopCo does not need thousands of random products to be useful. The goal is a dependable range of practical products with a clear reason to exist.

## Creative rules

For each post, create channel-specific variants for Facebook/Instagram, TikTok/Shorts, X and article/RSS reuse. Do not reuse identical copy everywhere. Named products require current evidence. Category education can run before a final SKU is cleared.
