# AgentOS × Content360 — Demographic Campaign Operating System

**Marketing task:** M-A035  
**Date:** 2026-09-15 AEST  
**Status:** PREPARED / OWNER-DIRECTED MARKETING SYSTEM / NO CAMPAIGN ACTIVATION

## North-star ambition
AgentOS is intended for every home, business, school and computer. Mass reach therefore requires one coherent brand with many evidence-disciplined campaign families rather than one generic advertisement.

## Master proposition
**One conversation. A whole AI team behind it.**

Supporting brand direction:
- **One AI to rule them all.**
- **Give AgentOS a real job.**
- **Stay in control. See what it did.**

The 17 specialist agents are a coordinated system behind the Overseer, not 17 competing user interfaces. The user always enters through the Overseer.

## Segmentation model
Content360 campaigns should be generated from:

`life context × age/life stage × expertise × primary problem × trust concern × desired outcome × platform × funnel stage`

### Critical rule
**Age/life stage is not expertise.**

Do not assume older users are novices or younger users are experts. Technical confidence must be modelled separately.

## Core audience families
1. Families / home
2. Children and younger students — only with age-appropriate, compliant framing and controls
3. Teenagers / senior students
4. Teachers / schools / education organisations
5. Everyday novice users
6. Older adults
7. Professionals / creators
8. Small business owners and teams
9. Larger organisations / IT / security / governance buyers
10. Developers / power users / Tech Heads

## Benefit pillars
Every campaign should deliberately weight one or more of:
- **Better answers** — quality floor, appropriate model/capability selection, verification where supported.
- **Safer actions** — authority, permissions, boundaries and evidence, only to the level actually proven.
- **Less complexity** — one Overseer relationship instead of making users choose every model/tool/agent manually.
- **Lower AI cost** — routing/budget discipline where evidenced; never invent savings.
- **Proof of what happened** — bounded canonical evidence, without overstating Green/PRS/Evidence Timeline maturity.

## Audience examples
### Family / home
Lead with control, privacy, answer quality, understandable permissions and household usefulness. Avoid unsupported `child-safe`, `guaranteed safe` or parental-control claims.

### Education
Lead with explanation, learning, research quality, evidence visibility and oversight. Do not market cheating, evasion of school rules or guaranteed educational outcomes.

### Novice
Lead with: `You do not need to know which AI, model or agent to choose. Start with the Overseer.` Avoid jargon-first creative.

### Older adult
Lead with simplicity, confidence, accessibility, scams/verification only where supported, and natural interaction. Do not stereotype age as incompetence.

### Professional / creator
Lead with quality, evidence, model/capability coordination, repeatable jobs and cost/time discipline.

### Small business
Lead with useful work, administration, governed delegation, evidence, cost control and reduced tool sprawl. Avoid employee-replacement or guaranteed-labour-saving claims.

### Enterprise / IT / security
Lead with authority, bounded permissions, evidence, provider neutrality, policy and assurance trajectory. Never imply certifications/compliance that are not proven.

### Developer / Tech Head
Lead with provider/model neutrality, capability routing, MCP-first architecture, evidence, budgets, policy and observability. Do not claim generic MCP/plugin support is unique differentiation.

## Content360 input contract
Every campaign asset should receive:
- approved claim ID / wording;
- evidence pointer and freshness;
- claim class: PROVEN / BETA-PROVEN / NEARLY PROVEN / PRODUCT DIRECTION / NOT YET SUPPORTABLE;
- target audience and expertise level;
- problem and desired outcome;
- platform and format;
- funnel stage;
- CTA;
- allowed wording;
- prohibited wording;
- whether human/platform-policy review is required.

Content360 must not upgrade a claim class or create product facts.

## Output status
Each generated asset should be classified:
- `ORGANIC_SAFE`
- `PAID_ELIGIBLE`
- `PAID_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `DO_NOT_PUBLISH`

Paid eligibility requires a fresh check against the current platform/ad rules before activation. An organic-safe claim is not automatically paid-ad eligible.

## 17-agent campaign treatment
Safe/product-direction framing:
- `One conversation. A whole AI team behind it.`
- `17 specialist agents, coordinated behind one Overseer experience.`
- `You ask once. AgentOS coordinates the specialists.`

Do not say:
- `17 autonomous employees`;
- all 17 agents work on every request;
- each specialist is a direct authority-bearing chat endpoint;
- each agent is independently safe/verified;
- names/personas are already customizable until implementation evidence exists.

## Voice treatment
Verbal conversation is **PRODUCT DIRECTION / NOT SHIPPED** until repository and acceptance evidence proves it. Campaign concepts may be prepared but must not say `Talk to AgentOS today`, `hands-free control`, or similar availability claims.

## Family / minors guard
For any campaign involving children or teens:
- use age-appropriate educational framing;
- avoid manipulative/exploitative targeting;
- avoid unsupported safety guarantees;
- keep parent/school control claims tied to current product evidence;
- require current platform and jurisdictional policy review before paid targeting.

## Learning loop
Performance may optimize hooks, formats, audiences and channel mix. It must not optimize by weakening truth. Winning copy remains bounded by the evidence pointer and claim class that authorized it.

## Activation gate
This operating system is ready for Content360 preparation, taxonomy and draft generation. It does **not** authorize public Founding Beta recruitment, paid campaigns, telemetry activation or unsupported product claims.

**Decision:** broad ambition should be marketed through tightly segmented campaigns while preserving one simple user relationship: the Overseer.
