## Prohibited lifecycle tactics
- fake countdowns;
- fake limited places;
- `You're missing out` without quantified/evidenced basis;
- manufactured degradation of Free;
- hidden AI Plus bundling;
- invented savings;
- fear-based security upsells;
- implying paid users receive more truthful Green/PRS results;
- repeated upgrade prompts after a clear decline without a material new trigger.
