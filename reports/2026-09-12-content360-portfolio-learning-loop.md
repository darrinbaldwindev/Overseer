# Content360 Portfolio Marketing Learning Loop — 2026-09-12

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** ACTIVE STANDARD

## Objective

Turn published content into evidence-backed learning for product, category, pricing, positioning and distribution decisions without allowing marketing metrics to overrule product/source evidence.

## Loop

1. Project workstream supplies verified facts / approved proposition.
2. Marketing creates campaign hypothesis.
3. Content360 distributes approved asset.
4. Performance receipt captures quantitative + qualitative response.
5. Marketing compares result against the campaign hypothesis and prior variants.
6. Result becomes one of:
   - creative learning;
   - audience/use-case learning;
   - product/category research priority;
   - landing-page/conversion learning;
   - claim/message learning;
   - no-action/noise.
7. Relevant project workstream verifies any product/programme implication.
8. Marketing updates the next content/experiment cycle.

## Decision boundaries

Marketing MAY recommend:
- more research on a high-interest category;
- creative/CTA changes;
- landing-page tests;
- audience/use-case emphasis;
- pausing weak messages;
- scaling evidence-backed winners.

Marketing MUST NOT conclude from social performance alone that:
- a supplier/product is viable;
- a programme is reputable/current;
- a feature is implemented;
- a safety claim is true;
- a franchise model is profitable;
- a price/offer is commercially sustainable.

## Project feedback examples

### AgentOS
Strong saves/comments on Free AI content → prioritise free-AI benchmark/proof and onboarding content; do not promote “effective using free AI alone” until benchmark clears.

### GlobalShopCo / Shopify→eBay
Strong engagement on Home Organisation → increase candidate research priority, but supplier/freight/margin assurance still controls SKU approval.

### Affiliate Websites
High clicks on user-testing content → research more verified user-testing programmes for that country; do not rank solely by commission.

### GhostKitchen / Franchise
Repeated questions about operating hours/unit economics → create educational explainer; do not convert interest into earnings claims.

## Review rhythm

- Weekly: creative/channel learning
- Monthly: category/message/landing-page learning
- Quarterly or major milestone: positioning/funnel review

Fast-moving commercial facts are reviewed under the separate evidence-expiry rules, not this cadence.

## Scale rule

A campaign becomes `SCALE-CANDIDATE` only when:
- performance exceeds an agreed baseline or comparable variants;
- the destination converts or meaningfully advances the funnel;
- underlying evidence remains current;
- scaling does not create unsupported claims or unsustainable economics;
- paid amplification, if considered, has its own budget/authority approval.
