## 3. Affiliate Websites — Australia

### Public evidence found

**Ipsos iSay Australia** currently documents a member “Refer a Friend” programme. The public FAQ states that an eligible member receives 500 points for a successful referral where the referred person joins, completes their profile and completes at least one survey. It also states the referred person must live in Australia, and rewards are capped at 10 successful referrals per month. The programme terms permit use of the unique referral link across websites/social media subject to programme rules and other site/platform restrictions.

Source URLs checked 2026-09-12:
- https://www.ipsosisay.com/en-au/faq-refer-a-friend
- https://www.ipsosisay.com/en-au/refer-a-friend-tc

Prime Opinion also has a live Australian site advertising paid surveys with cash/gift-card rewards, but this pass did not verify an Australian publisher affiliate programme for Prime Opinion.

### Marketing decision

**Status: PARTIALLY UNBLOCKED.**

Safe editorial content:
- Ipsos iSay exists in Australia;
- members can earn survey points;
- the current Australian member referral programme and its published 500-point successful-referral rule, with date/terms qualification.

Not yet safe to describe as our website’s publisher affiliate programme unless the Affiliate Websites workstream has a valid, approved publisher/referral account/path and verifies that its use complies with Ipsos terms.

Important distinction: **member referral programme ≠ publisher affiliate programme by default.**
