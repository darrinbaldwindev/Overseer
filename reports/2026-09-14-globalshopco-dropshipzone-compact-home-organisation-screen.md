## Exact candidate admission contract
No product enters the pilot matrix until all of the following exist for that exact SKU:
- exact supplier + SKU/EAN/MPN where available;
- canonical pack count/model/variant identity with no contradictory feed data;
- actual dimensions and shipping weight/cubic basis;
- current AU stock evidence;
- actual retailer/account acquisition cost and GST treatment;
- exact freight for representative AU destinations;
- returns/warranty terms;
- seller/packing identity and buyer-data handling;
- eBay ownership/marketplace compatibility;
- realistic AU/eBay delivered-price comps;
- positive free-delivery contribution after applicable eBay/integration costs.
