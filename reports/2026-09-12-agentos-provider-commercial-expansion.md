## Purpose

Durably record the latest Marketing Overseer expansion of the AgentOS provider/infrastructure catalogue and the associated commercial-income model, while clearly separating verified provider facts from hypotheses and unsupported commercial assumptions.

## Provider / infrastructure additions carried forward

The following provider and infrastructure categories are now recommended for the AgentOS Dynamic Capability Registry / verification queue:

- **Mistral AI** — direct API/SDK; strong provider diversity, lower-cost models, document/OCR/audio capability; useful for general intelligence, document processing and fallback routing.
- **Cloudflare Workers AI** — Workers AI API/bindings; useful for free/low-cost inference capacity, edge execution, gateway integration and lightweight workloads.
- **Cerebras Inference** — OpenAI-compatible API; high-throughput inference candidate for low-latency agent loops, extraction, classification and reasoning.
- **Together AI** — broad open-model cloud catalogue with text/vision/image/audio/video/embedding/reranking/moderation capabilities; useful as open-model cloud supplier and specialist-modality fallback.
- **Fireworks AI** — serverless plus dedicated deployment path; useful for open-model experimentation, specialist models and scalable managed inference.
- **Hugging Face Inference Providers** — unified provider abstraction layer with BYOK; strategically useful as secondary provider/fallback abstraction but must not replace AgentOS's own canonical registry/routing authority.
- **xAI / Grok API** — OpenAI-compatible API; additional frontier-model family and provider-diversity option.
- **NVIDIA NIM** — hosted development APIs / self-deployed NIM containers; future enterprise/private-inference and controlled-GPU deployment path.

- provider/model fallback;
- free-model routing;
- BYOK;
- consolidated billing/usage;
- application attribution.

However, the earlier claim that ordinary AgentOS applications can simply set `HTTP-Referer` / `X-OpenRouter-Title` and add a configurable **2%–5% developer markup** to user token spend is **not accepted as verified**.

**Current classification:** `UNKNOWN — COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT REQUIRED / VERIFY BEFORE CLAIMING`.

Those headers should be treated as app attribution / identification unless a separate current OpenRouter commercial arrangement explicitly authorises revenue share or markup.

Affiliate or partner economics must never alter technical capability ranking or routing priority.

## Revised AgentOS income strategy

### 1. AgentOS software revenue is primary

AgentOS should not depend on provider affiliate commissions for viability.

Working commercial ladder remains:

- Free;
- **$39/year Co-worker challenger** — validation hypothesis, not final pricing;
- **$99/year Operator**;
- higher-capacity / specialist / team / business subscriptions;
- BYO AI / specialist capability where applicable.

### 2. BYOK

Users may connect their own provider credentials/subscriptions where terms permit, including OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter, xAI, Together and others.

AgentOS monetises its **orchestration, governance, workflow, assurance, recovery and autonomy software**, not by pretending the user's own API spend is AgentOS revenue.

### 3. Managed AI capacity

Future possibility:

**AgentOS subscription + included AI allowance + metered additional usage**.

This is a commercial hypothesis requiring real unit-economic modelling, provider terms, billing infrastructure and margin validation before launch.

### 4. Gateway / routing service

Potential advanced/team paid service:

- unified provider billing;
- policy-controlled routing;
- budget controls;
- failover;
- observability;
- usage reporting;
- provider abstraction;
- organisation-level model permissions.

This can be valuable independently of affiliate commission.

### 5. Verified partner / affiliate revenue

Affiliate income may be used only where there is an **actual, current, verified programme**.

Potential categories:

- hosting;
- automation platforms;
- SaaS integrations;
- specialist AI tools;
- deployment services;
- complementary software.

Commission percentages must not be assumed from category norms.

Affiliate status must never change AgentOS technical recommendation/routing priority.

### 6. Local / free AI as product advantage

Free/local inference is strategically valuable even without direct affiliate income because it can:

- reduce delivery cost;
- make Free genuinely useful;
- extend paid-plan capacity;
- reduce unnecessary premium consumption;
- support local/private execution;
- improve resilience/provider diversity.

Safe marketing language:

> **AgentOS can make use of free, paid and local AI according to the job, your settings and the capability available.**

Do not promise guaranteed savings.
