## Marketing readiness states

### RED — no public product promotion

Any SKU with:

- marketplace permission UNKNOWN or denied;
- freight UNKNOWN where free-delivery economics depend on it;
- trade cost UNKNOWN;
- stock/ownership model UNKNOWN;
- seller-identity/packing UNKNOWN;
- returns/warranty UNKNOWN;
- eBay-inclusive contribution UNKNOWN/non-positive.

### AMBER — creative may be prepared but not activated

A SKU may enter AMBER only when:

- exact supplier + SKU identity is verified;
- marketplace/eBay permission is evidenced or explicitly pending under a known approval process;
- representative freight basis exists;
- Australian retail comparison exists;
- intended fulfilment path is documented.

AMBER permits:

- draft copy;
- image/creative templates using approved supplier assets where rights are clear;
- channel taxonomy/category planning;
- SEO keyword preparation;
- campaign naming/UTM preparation;
- product-page skeletons with no public availability claim.

### GREEN FOR MARKETING ACTIVATION — requires owner/channel approval

Before product-specific eBay/paid/social activation, require evidence of:

1. written marketplace/eBay permission where supplier requires it;
2. authorised trade cost;
3. stock/ownership/ordering compatibility;
4. seller identity and packing-slip compatibility;
5. buyer-data handling compatibility;
6. inventory synchronisation/buffer plan;
7. final parcel/cubic freight treatment;
8. returns/warranty path;
9. eBay fees + integration allowance;
10. positive conservative free-delivery contribution;
11. owner approval for channel activation/listing publication.

Marketing GREEN is channel/content readiness only. It is not overall project GREEN.

## First-pilot message architecture — PREP ONLY

When 5–10 pilot SKUs pass the evidence gate, use a simple proposition:

### Customer promise

- straightforward product information;
- delivery included in displayed price only where economics/supporting policy are actually confirmed;
- clear returns/warranty information;
- GlobalShopCo remains seller/customer-service identity where fulfilment model supports that representation.

### eBay listing copy structure

1. Exact product name/model.
2. Three to five verified customer-benefit bullets.
3. Exact dimensions/material/specification from evidence.
4. What is included.
5. Delivery statement based on approved freight model.
6. Returns/warranty statement based on confirmed process.
7. No false scarcity, unsupported bestseller language or invented supplier claims.

### Social launch structure

- Product problem/use case first.
- Exact product benefit second.
- Price/delivery only after current evidence refresh.
- CTA to the live approved listing only after publication authority exists.
