**Intent:** informational / problem-solving.

### Cluster article 1 — Small pantry reset
**SEO title:** Small Pantry Organisation: A Practical Reset Before You Buy Storage

**Primary query themes:** small pantry organisation, pantry storage ideas, organise small pantry.

**Answer-first intro:** Start by measuring shelf width, usable depth and clearance around doors or hinges. The right organiser is the one that fits the actual space and makes frequently used items easier to reach.

**FAQ candidates:**
- How do I organise a small pantry with deep shelves?
- Should pantry organisers be clear or opaque?
- What measurements should I take before buying pantry storage?

### Cluster article 2 — Fridge bins
**SEO title:** Are Fridge Storage Bins Worth It? What to Check Before Buying

**Primary query themes:** fridge storage bins, refrigerator organiser bins, clear fridge containers.

**Answer-first intro:** Fridge bins work best when they match shelf depth, leave airflow and door clearance, and group items you actually use together. More bins are not automatically better.

**FAQ candidates:**
- Do fridge bins reduce usable fridge space?
- Are lids useful on refrigerator organisers?
- How should I measure a fridge shelf for storage bins?

### Cluster article 3 — Under-sink
**SEO title:** Under-Sink Organisation: Measure Around Pipes Before Choosing Storage

**Primary query themes:** under sink organiser, bathroom under sink storage, kitchen sink storage ideas.

**Answer-first intro:** Under-sink storage is constrained by plumbing, traps, hoses and cabinet-door hardware. Measure the obstructions first; then choose around the space that actually remains.

**FAQ candidates:**
- How do I measure for an under-sink organiser?
- Are sliding under-sink drawers useful?
- What should not be stored under a sink?

### Cluster article 4 — Drawers
**SEO title:** Drawer Organisers: A Simple Fit Test Before You Buy

**Primary query themes:** drawer organiser, kitchen drawer storage, adjustable drawer organiser.

**Answer-first intro:** A drawer organiser only helps when its outside dimensions, compartment sizes and drawer clearance match what you use every day. Measure both the drawer and the objects before choosing a layout.

**FAQ candidates:**
- How much clearance does a drawer organiser need?
- Are adjustable organisers better than fixed trays?
- How do I stop drawer organisers moving?

### Trust article — free delivery economics
**SEO title:** What “Free Delivery” Really Means When Shopping Online

**Intent:** trust/education, not a store promise.

**Safe message:** Delivery is still a real cost even when a shopper is not charged a separate shipping line. A sustainable retailer has to account for product cost, freight, fees, returns and service inside the delivered economics.

**Do not write:** GlobalShopCo currently offers free delivery on any named product unless canonical product economics and store configuration prove it at publication time.
