# AgentOS — Tier Benefit Claim Matrix

**Date:** 2026-09-16 AEST  
**Task:** M-A048  
**Status:** COMPLETE / MARKETING CONTRACT / IMPLEMENTATION-GATED

## Canonical commercial direction
Current owner-updated product direction remains:
- Free — $0/year
- Standard — $49/year
- Advanced / Pro — $99/year
- AI Plus — $22/month, separate optional intelligence subscription
- Commercial / Business — TBD

These are product-direction targets, not proof of live billing or entitlements.

## Marketing principle
Free must be genuinely useful. Paid tiers should earn the upgrade by delivering evidenced additional value rather than by making the free experience deliberately frustrating.

## Benefit architecture
| Tier | Marketing role | Safe benefit direction | Evidence requirement before availability claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Useful entry AgentOS | core useful AgentOS experience; access to appropriate free/local/user-provided intelligence where supported | exact entitlement + runtime + UI evidence |
| Standard | Serious mainstream entry | additional convenience/capacity/capability that has clear everyday value | canonical tier entitlement mapping and enforcement |
| Advanced / Pro | Full personal / advanced use | deeper orchestration, controls, advanced capability and evidence surfaces where implemented | canonical entitlement + exact implementation proof |
| AI Plus | Optional intelligence resource | additional intelligence capacity/capability separate from AgentOS control-plane entitlement | subscription/provider/resource contract + live entitlement evidence |
| Commercial / Business | Organisational path | governance, multi-seat/admin, isolation, customer-controlled infrastructure and organisational controls where implemented | canonical business architecture + entitlement evidence; price remains TBD |

## Hard claim rules
Do not invent which exact features are paid until the canonical entitlement model says so.
Do not describe Free as a demo unless canonical product direction changes.
Do not imply Standard or Pro makes unsafe/unverified capabilities safe merely because they are paid.
Do not bundle AI Plus into AgentOS tier pricing in copy.
Do not claim unlimited usage without an explicit current limit contract.
Do not advertise savings versus external AI subscriptions without current like-for-like evidence.
Do not advertise Commercial/Business pricing until owner-approved.

## Upgrade messaging
Preferred structure:
1. what the user is already accomplishing;
2. the observed limitation/opportunity;
3. the specific paid capability that would address it;
4. expected benefit with evidence/assumptions;
5. price;
6. legitimate `stay Free` option.

Avoid fear, artificial urgency and deliberately obscured limits.

## Audience emphasis
- Novice: usefulness and simplicity first; tier mechanics second.
- Household: value, control and understandable limits.
- Professional: recurring usefulness and time/capability benefit.
- Developer/Tech Head: exact entitlement/capability boundaries.
- Business: governance and organisational controls, not consumer-tier feature inflation.

## Promotion gate
Before any feature appears in a public tier comparison as `included`, require a canonical source identifying the tier plus implementation evidence that the feature exists at the claimed level.

Unknown mapping must render internally as `TIER_MAPPING_UNKNOWN`, not inferred from product desirability.

## Disposition
**Pricing architecture is clear; detailed feature allocation remains evidence-gated. Marketing can build the value story without inventing entitlements.**
