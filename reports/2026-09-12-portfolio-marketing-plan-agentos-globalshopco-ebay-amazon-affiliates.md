# Portfolio Marketing Plan — AgentOS, GlobalShopCo, Shopify→eBay, Shopify→Amazon & Affiliate Websites

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** ACTIVE / EVIDENCE-GATED  
**Distribution:** Content360 Overseer

## Portfolio objective

Build five commercially distinct but operationally coordinated marketing engines:

1. AgentOS
2. GlobalShopCo
3. Shopify → eBay
4. Shopify → Amazon
5. Affiliate Websites

Shared infrastructure should handle content production, Content360 distribution, analytics, creative testing, SEO/AEO principles, audience research, conversion testing, evidence/claims control, campaign IDs, reusable creative production and cross-project learning.

Each project retains its own proposition, offer, customer, landing pages, conversion event, unit economics and evidence requirements.

## Priority order

### P1 — AgentOS
Highest strategic upside. Objective: awareness → useful-job activation → $99 Operator conversion.

### P1 — GlobalShopCo
Immediate ecommerce/commercial engine. Objective: find winning products/categories → acquire profitably → improve repeat purchase and basket value.

### P1 — Shopify → eBay
Marketplace expansion. Objective: identify products well suited to eBay demand and convert through trusted, evidence-cleared listings.

### P2 — Affiliate Websites
Lower inventory/capital risk. Objective: build authoritative country-specific traffic → help users find worthwhile reward programmes → convert through affiliate/referral programmes.

### P2 — Shopify → Amazon
Not yet set up. Objective now: research and prepare the channel before committing inventory, integrations or advertising budget.

## Shared marketing system

Research → Evidence → Positioning → Content → Distribution → Traffic → Conversion → Analytics → Learning → Better product/campaign selection.

Content360 is the distribution layer, not the source of truth for product facts, prices, availability, supplier data, programme terms, positioning, commercial priorities or evidence state.

# AgentOS

## Primary positioning

Hero: **Put AI to work.**

Supporting: **You already have AI. AgentOS helps you make the most of it.**

Economic story: **Use free AI. Use local AI. Use the AI you already pay for. AgentOS helps put it all to work together.**

Operator: **$99/year — less than $2 a week.**

Commercial story: save time + make better use of existing/free AI + reduce unnecessary AI spend. Do not guarantee savings until measured.

## Audiences

- everyday/home users
- parents/families
- students
- professionals
- small businesses
- creators
- developers
- AI power users

## Content pillars

1. Free AI
2. Give AgentOS a real job
3. Meet Willow / Isla / Jack / Henry
4. Operator economics / “Would this save you $2 a week?”
5. Trust / approvals / limits / evidence / privacy
6. Night Shift when runtime evidence supports the claim

## Funnel

Awareness → Interest → Free activation → Value proof → $39/$99 upgrade → Referral after demonstrated value.

## Paid channels

Initial: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, creator/influencer, retargeting.

Test: Free AI vs Put AI to work vs <$2/week vs Stop juggling AI vs AI team on your computer.

## North-star / KPIs

Primary: activated users completing useful jobs.

Secondary: Free→$39, Free→$99, $39→$99, CAC, retention, jobs/user, measured time saved, measured AI spend avoided, referral rate.

# GlobalShopCo

## Position

A store for useful, carefully selected products rather than a huge generic dropshipping catalogue.

## Category strategy

Concentrate where demand, practical usefulness, reliable suppliers, manageable returns, acceptable landed margin and free-delivery economics are strongest.

Current themes: home organisation, pet, baby, safety, household utility.

## Content pillars

- problem → solution
- useful finds
- comparisons
- selection transparency
- trust: delivery/returns/warranty
- useful editorial guides via headless/WordPress

## Funnel

Content/social/search → article/category → product page → Shopify checkout → post-purchase → related products → repeat customer.

## SEO/AEO

Problem-led pages, e.g. small pantry organisation, pet travel, childproofing, home storage, practical gifts.

## KPIs

Gross margin after freight, conversion rate, CAC, AOV, repeat purchase, return rate, CTR by product/category, contribution margin, email capture, category profitability.

No scale until freight economics are known.

eBay is a marketplace distribution channel, not just a Shopify clone. Winning eBay SKUs may differ from website winners.

## Current category universe

2. Pet Accessories
3. Kids Storage & Organisation
4. Kitchen & Pantry Organisation
5. Mobile & Computer Accessories
6. Garden & Outdoor Utility
7. Baby & Home Safety
8. Automotive Accessories
9. Fitness & Exercise Accessories
10. Cleaning & Household Utility

## Strategy

Three layers:
- search demand: listing title/category/item specifics/images
- marketplace trust: price, shipping clarity, returns, seller credibility
- external demand: Content360 social to selected verified listings

## Content types

Before/after, demonstrations, useful finds, category comparisons, “worth it?” videos, buyer guides, seasonal use cases, practical household problems.

## Qualification flow

Research → Candidate → Economics verified → Listing verified → Content-ready → Promoted → Measured → Scale / Hold / Reject.

## KPIs

Impressions, listing CTR, sell-through, conversion, eBay fees, landed contribution, return rate, listing rank, promoted-listing cost where used, social-assisted sales, category performance.

# Shopify → Amazon

## Status

**PRE-SETUP / RESEARCH.**

Do not treat Amazon as operational yet.

## Phase 1 — Research

Determine:
- Amazon Australia first vs other marketplaces
- seller account requirements
- Shopify integration options
- FBA vs FBM
- product/category restrictions
- referral fees
- fulfilment/storage costs
- returns
- advertising costs
- GTIN/barcode requirements
- brand/IP exposure
- dropshipping compliance
- supplier compatibility

## Phase 2 — Product suitability

Score GlobalShopCo/eBay candidates by:
- Amazon search demand
- competition/review moat
- Buy Box feasibility
- price compression
- fees/FBA/FBM costs
- return exposure
- dimensions/weight
- restricted-category risk
- IP/brand restrictions
- margin after Amazon costs

Classify SKUs as Shopify-only, eBay-first, Amazon-first or multichannel. Do not force every SKU everywhere.

## Phase 3 — Pilot

Pilot approximately 10–20 high-confidence products, then measure before expansion.

## Amazon marketing emphasis

Strong listing, keyword research, imagery, A+ content where available, policy-compliant review generation, competitive offer, Amazon Ads only after baseline economics are known.

## KPIs

Amazon contribution margin, organic rank, ACoS, TACoS, conversion, Buy Box share, returns, review trajectory, fulfilment/storage cost, inventory turns, channel-specific profit.

# Affiliate Websites

## Structure

Master platform + Australia + United Kingdom + United States.

Each country keeps separate marketing/compliance.

## Main proposition

Help people find legitimate ways to earn rewards, cash or benefits from reputable programmes.

User benefit comes before affiliate payout.

## Core categories

Paid surveys, focus groups, user testing, product testing, research studies, mystery shopping, cashback/rewards, referral programmes and other legitimate paid-participation opportunities.

## Content model

- high-intent SEO
- comparison content
- educational / realistic earning guidance
- programme profiles
- country/reward/opportunity roundups

## Content360 model

WordPress → RSS → Content360 → country social feed → article → verified programme → affiliate/referral conversion.

Prefer social → trusted site/comparison → programme, not direct social → affiliate link by default.

## Country emphasis

Australia: surveys, research, cashback, focus groups, testing.

UK: initially non-financial rewards/research, with extra scrutiny for FCA/ASA-sensitive content.

US: broader paid-participation ecosystem including surveys, focus groups, testing and expert/research platforms.

## KPIs

Organic traffic, rankings, programme CTR, signup conversion, earnings/visitor, earnings/click, content freshness, email subscribers, return users, country/category RPM, reputation/complaints.

Do not split spend equally.

# 90-day plan

## Days 1–30 — Foundation

AgentOS: Free-AI / Put-AI-to-work framework.

GlobalShopCo: identify evidence-cleared products and strongest categories.

eBay: finish 10-category candidate set and test listings.

Amazon: research integration/channel economics only.

Affiliate: publish strong country-specific comparison and programme pages.

Portfolio: Content360 groups, analytics taxonomy, campaign IDs and claim controls.

## Days 31–60 — Test

AgentOS: test Free AI vs Operator economics vs real jobs.

GlobalShopCo: test products/categories/creative.

eBay: identify strongest listing/category conversion.

Amazon: select pilot shortlist.

Affiliate: identify highest-converting programme categories and keywords.

Kill weak campaigns quickly.

## Days 61–90 — Scale

Scale only behind evidence:
- best AgentOS positioning
- best ecommerce categories
- best eBay listings
- Amazon pilot if economics qualify
- highest-EPC affiliate content

Begin creator/influencer scaling for AgentOS and expand SEO clusters for Affiliate Websites and GlobalShopCo.
