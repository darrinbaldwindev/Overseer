## Publication rule

No specific product post is publishable until the eBay/GlobalShopCo workstream has verified enough material facts for that post: exact product/SKU or listing identity, current price, current availability, freight/delivery basis, returns/warranty claims where used, image rights, and any safety/material claims.

Where product evidence is incomplete, publish category-level educational content only; do not invent or imply availability.

## Channel priority

Primary: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest-style visual content where supported, X for short updates, and eBay-linked landing/link-in-bio pages. LinkedIn is low priority except for seller/build-in-public content.

## Content formats

Each product/category idea should generate a reusable package:
- 1 square/portrait carousel;
- 1 vertical 15–30 second video;
- 1 short-caption version;
- 1 longer Facebook/Instagram caption;
- 1 product/category landing blurb;
- 1 link-in-bio entry;
- source/evidence record.

## Product promotion ladder

**Level 0 — Research only:** not publishable.  
**Level 1 — Category education:** publish category/problem content, no exact product claim.  
**Level 2 — Candidate product:** may appear in non-promotional comparison only if facts are clearly qualified.  
**Level 3 — Evidence-cleared:** product spotlight permitted.  
**Level 4 — Live listing verified:** direct conversion CTA permitted while evidence remains current.

## Metrics

Track by category and creative:
- saves;
- shares;
- click-through rate;
- product-page/eBay clicks;
- comments/questions;
- video completion;
- conversion where attributable;
- evidence expiry / listing drift.

Do not use engagement alone to keep a product live if listing, freight, supplier or returns evidence has become stale.
