# Affiliate Websites — Verified Programme Candidate Register

**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Owner:** Marketing Overseer
**Status:** ACTIVE / EVIDENCE-DATED / COUNTRY-SAFE

## Purpose

Separate three programme types that must not be conflated in Affiliate Websites or Content360:

1. **Publisher affiliate programme** — commercial publisher/network relationship capable of paying the site for referred qualifying actions.
2. **Member referral programme** — reward available to an ordinary member for referring people, not automatically a publisher monetisation right.
3. **Editorial-only rewards platform** — useful to users but no current verified referral/affiliate monetisation route for this project.

## Evidence date

All web evidence below was checked on 2026-09-12. Dynamic programme terms must be reverified before publication when the evidence-expiry rules require it.

## Australia

### Ipsos iSay Australia

**Classification:** MEMBER REFERRAL / EDITORIAL CANDIDATE

**Verified facts:**
- Ipsos iSay Australia currently operates a Refer a Friend programme.
- The member receives 500 points for each successful referral.
- A successful referral requires the referred friend to join, complete their profile and complete at least one survey.
- Referred friends must be 18+ and live in Australia.
- Rewards are capped at 10 successful referrals per month.

**Commercial status for Affiliate Websites:**
- Do **not** treat the ordinary member referral programme as a publisher-affiliate programme.
- Editorial coverage is permitted in principle if terms remain current.
- Monetised site-wide distribution requires a separately verified publisher/commercial arrangement.

**Public-safe wording:**
"Ipsos iSay Australia currently offers eligible members 500 points for a successful friend referral, subject to its current programme terms."

**Source:** Ipsos iSay Australia Refer a Friend + programme terms.

### PrizeRebel

**Classification:** GLOBAL REFERRAL/AFFILIATE CANDIDATE

**Verified facts:**
- PrizeRebel publicly describes its referral offering as an affiliate programme.
- It says affiliates/referrers can earn a percentage of referral earnings, with the percentage depending on account level.
- PrizeRebel states it is available globally and specifically references the US, UK, Canada and Australia among core countries.
- Survey availability varies by geography/demographics.
- Rewards include gift cards and cash-style redemption methods depending on market.

**Commercial status for Affiliate Websites:**
- Candidate for AU editorial + monetised testing, but actual site/account eligibility, referral link rights and traffic rules must be checked before launch.
- Do not publish the historical "up to 30%" as a guaranteed default rate without reconfirming the current account-level table.

## United States

### SurveyRewards US via Awin

**Classification:** VERIFIED PUBLISHER-AFFILIATE CANDIDATE

**Verified programme terms:**
- Awin programme: SurveyRewards US.
- Commission stated as USD $0.75 per valid survey complete.
- Commission can be generated for the first 20 completed surveys per unique referred user, for a stated maximum potential of USD $15 per unique user.
- User must be real/unique, new to SurveyRewards, successfully complete the survey process and not use proxy/VPN/bot traffic.
- Email, social media, content/blog and general web traffic are listed as allowed traffic.
- Incentivised traffic is prohibited.
- PPC requires written approval; brand bidding is prohibited.
- Provided creative assets should be used, and false earnings claims are prohibited.

**Commercial status:**
- Strong candidate for US Affiliate Websites.
- Actual monetised deployment still requires publisher acceptance/approval in Awin and compliance with programme terms.

### QuickRewards via Awin

**Classification:** VERIFIED PUBLISHER-AFFILIATE CANDIDATE

**Verified programme facts:**
- Awin programme ID 87781.
- QuickRewards describes itself as a free rewards membership platform.
- The Awin profile states a publisher is paid USD $1 when the referred visitor registers and earns at least USD $0.25.
- Attribution period shown as 30 days.
- Higher rates may be available for high-volume affiliates.

**Commercial status:**
- Strong second US candidate.
- Publisher/network approval still required before monetised use.

## Cross-country editorial candidate

### Swagbucks

**Classification:** MEMBER REFERRAL / EDITORIAL CANDIDATE

**Verified facts:**
- Swagbucks supports members in Australia, the United Kingdom and the United States among other countries.
- Current referral help material states a referrer receives 10% of eligible referral earnings; some referral bonuses vary by country.

**Commercial status:**
- Do not assume an ordinary member referral account is suitable for publisher-site monetisation.
- Useful editorial candidate in AU/UK/US; publisher rights require separate verification.
