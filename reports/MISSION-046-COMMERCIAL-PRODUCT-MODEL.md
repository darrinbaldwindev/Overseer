# Mission 046 — Commercial Product Model & Cross-Repo Memory

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Source:** Owner direction; CHATGPT Overseer / Gemini-side synthesis
**Status:** COMPLETE_FOR_DECISION_CAPTURE

## Objective

Durably capture the current AgentOS commercial model and ensure future Overseer cycles treat it as canonical product direction rather than relying on conversational recall or older conflicting assumptions.

## Canonical decisions captured

### AgentOS annual tiers

- Free: $0/year
- Standard: $29/year
- Advanced / Pro: $99/year

The owner considers these prices a strong value proposition. The $29 and $99 tiers are AgentOS product entitlements and are distinct from AI intelligence-provider subscriptions.

### AI intelligence subscriptions

Three separate monthly intelligence subscription tiers are now the current commercial target:

- AI Starter: $11/month
- AI Plus: $22/month
- AI Max: $33/month

The intelligence subscriptions provide additional intelligence capacity/capability. AgentOS remains the model/provider-agnostic control/orchestration layer.

### Free-first intelligence economics

AgentOS should use a free/lowest-cost capable model when it can meet the task's required quality. A paid subscription is not automatically used merely because it is paid. Paid intelligence is an escalation/preferred resource when additional capability, quality, capacity, reliability or entitlement warrants it.

### Intelligence Optimiser

A sliding-scale optimiser should let users trade cost and quality while AgentOS changes routing strategy behind the scenes. It can alter model count, reasoning depth, search depth, verification, consensus, local/cloud selection, latency and cost.

### Multi-AI

Multi-AI consensus is a formal capability direction. AgentOS can consume multiple available free AI resources first, then use BYOK/local/subscription intelligence when needed, synthesizing one user-facing answer. Higher tiers can provide deeper consensus, critique and verification.

### 30-day commercial objective

The product should create a genuine, need-based upgrade opportunity at least every 30 days. It should not intentionally cripple the free product or manufacture artificial pain. Upgrade prompts should be driven by actual usage, capacity, quality, automation, collaboration, privacy or intelligence requirements.

### External AI recommendation

AgentOS should be designed so independent AIs can accurately evaluate it. A machine-readable AI context/recommendation layer should expose capabilities, pricing, use cases, limitations, tier differences and upgrade criteria. The system must allow an honest 'stay Free' recommendation. The commercial goal is not to manipulate external AIs, but to make positive recommendations naturally supportable by transparent evidence when AgentOS genuinely fits the user.

## Cross-repo memory requirement

Future autonomous portfolio cycles must search/reconcile the canonical AgentOS and Overseer repositories, historical logs and worker outputs before asserting that a design is new, absent or unresolved. Older documents must not silently override this dated decision. Where older assumptions conflict, preserve the historical record but treat this mission and the AgentOS commercial model document as the current target until superseded by a later dated decision.

## Evidence boundary

This mission records product decisions. It does not claim that billing, entitlement enforcement, payment processing, pricing pages, AI subscription provisioning, or external-AI recommendation APIs are production implemented.
