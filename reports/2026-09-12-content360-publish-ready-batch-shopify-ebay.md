## Creative direction

- Marketplace-clean rather than bargain-bin styling.
- Avoid fake urgency, strikethrough prices and unsupported “best seller” badges.
- Comparison/education posts can publish before SKU clearance; direct-response SKU posts cannot.
- Every live listing creative gets a source ID and expiry/recheck date.
