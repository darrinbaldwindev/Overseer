## Priority 0 — Infrastructure / publisher identity

### Awin publisher account

Current Awin publisher guidance supports websites, social media accounts, newsletters, blogs, videos and other content publishers that recommend products/services and use tracked affiliate links. Awin states publisher participation is free and its advertiser directory contains a large number of brands/programmes.

**Status:** HIGH PRIORITY / ACCOUNT REQUIRED BEFORE AWIN PROGRAM APPLICATIONS

**Reason:** SurveyRewards US and QuickRewards are current Awin programme candidates, and Awin is also a strong discovery layer for future AU/UK/US offers.

**Before setup/application:**
- define the primary Affiliate Websites domain to present as publisher property;
- have privacy policy, affiliate disclosure, contact page and basic editorial content live;
- define AU/UK/US market structure clearly;
- prepare short publisher description and traffic acquisition explanation;
- do not promise volume that does not exist yet.

## Priority 1 — Strongest current publisher-affiliate candidates

### SurveyRewards US — Awin

**Market:** United States  
**Type:** TRUE PUBLISHER-AFFILIATE PROGRAMME  
**Current evidence:** $0.75 USD per valid survey completion; first 20 completed surveys per unique user; social media, email, content/blogs and general web traffic listed as allowed; incentivised traffic prohibited; PPC requires written approval; brand bidding prohibited.

**Commercial fit:** HIGH for US surveys/rewards site because commission is tied to completed survey activity rather than only signup.

**Application readiness:** HIGH once publisher infrastructure exists.

**Content360 restriction:** Social posts can promote editorial content around SurveyRewards only after programme acceptance if the destination is a monetised tracking link. Before acceptance, editorial-only mention is allowed if factual and non-affiliate.

### QuickRewards — Awin

**Market:** Primarily US; verify any other market before promotion.  
**Type:** TRUE PUBLISHER-AFFILIATE PROGRAMME  
**Current evidence:** $1 once the referred visitor registers and earns at least $0.25; 30-day attribution period; higher rates may be available for high-volume affiliates.

**Commercial fit:** HIGH/MEDIUM. Easier conversion event than deeper survey-completion programmes, but final traffic and geographic terms must be checked in account before deployment.

**Application readiness:** HIGH once Awin publisher identity exists.

### PrizeRebel

**Markets:** Global, including AU/UK/US availability signals.  
**Type:** MEMBER-REFERRAL / DIRECT AFFILIATE HYBRID  
**Current evidence:** Referral page describes up to 30% bonus based on referral earnings and worldwide earning opportunities; application asks about website/promotion method.

**Commercial fit:** HIGH as a cross-country candidate, but separate publisher/application rights from ordinary member-referral use.

**Application readiness:** MEDIUM/HIGH.

**Required before monetised deployment:**
- confirm terms applicable to the exact publisher promotion method;
- confirm whether one account/link may be used across AU/UK/US or separate treatment is required;
- preserve user-facing disclosure.

## Priority 2 — Network expansion

### Awin advertiser discovery

Use Awin advertiser search after publisher account approval to identify reward, survey, cashback, user-testing and research-participation programmes available to AU, UK and US publishers.

Rank opportunities by:
1. user reward value;
2. reputation;
3. target-country eligibility;
4. publisher acceptance terms;
5. conversion event quality;
6. traffic restrictions;
7. payout structure;
8. data-feed/deep-link capability;
9. disclosure/compliance burden;
10. programme stability.

Do not rank solely by commission.

## Application pack to prepare

Before any publisher-network application, prepare:
- publisher/site name;
- live domain;
- audience description;
- country coverage;
- content categories;
- traffic sources;
- social profiles if used;
- privacy policy;
- affiliate disclosure;
- contact details;
- sample editorial pages;
- explanation of how users benefit;
- statement that incentivised/fraudulent traffic is not used;
- expected early traffic stated conservatively;
- Content360 described only as a distribution/scheduling tool, not as an automated traffic-generation scheme.
