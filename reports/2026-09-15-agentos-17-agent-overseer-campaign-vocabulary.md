# AgentOS — 17-Agent / Overseer Campaign Vocabulary

**Date:** 2026-09-15 AEST  
**Marketing task:** M-A038  
**Status:** COMPLETE / CLAIM-SAFE VOCABULARY / NOT A SHIPMENT CLAIM

## Purpose
Give Marketing, Content360 and Frontend one vocabulary for explaining the owner-directed 17-agent model without turning product direction into implementation claims.

## Core model
The user relationship is with the **Overseer**. Specialist agents operate behind that relationship. Stable machine/system identity and controlled role are distinct from user-facing persona.

> **Identity is stable. Role is controlled. Persona is replaceable.**

> **One relationship with the Overseer; specialist roles behind it.**

## Claim-safe hierarchy

### Safe as owner-approved PRODUCT DIRECTION
- AgentOS is designed around one Overseer relationship coordinating specialist roles.
- The product direction includes 17 specialist agent roles.
- Names, appearances and voices are intended to be customisable independently of underlying role and authority where implemented.
- Willow, Isla, Jack and Henry are default personas/brand characters rather than authority identities.
- Persona changes must not change permissions, authority, evidence identity, Green or PRS semantics.

### Safe as current evidence-backed positioning
- AgentOS is being built around governed AI execution rather than unrestricted computer access.
- Current development separates authority, execution evidence, readiness and assurance instead of collapsing them into one success label.
- The Overseer is the intended user-facing coordination layer.

### Do not claim yet
- `17 autonomous employees`
- `17 agents work on every request`
- `all 17 agents are shipped/available`
- `talk directly to any specialist with full authority`
- `rename an agent and its permissions follow the new persona`
- `every agent independently verifies its own work`
- `voice personas are available now`
- `the team safely runs your whole computer autonomously`

## Campaign vocabulary by audience

### Simple / household
Preferred: `Tell the Overseer what you want done. AgentOS can coordinate the right specialist role behind the scenes while keeping control and evidence separate.`

Avoid internal role counts unless useful. Do not make the customer learn an org chart before understanding the product.

### Essentials / professional
Preferred: `One AI relationship, specialist roles behind it, with authority and evidence kept explicit.`

Use role examples only when they clarify value: planning, execution, guarding permissions, assurance.

### Tech Head / developer
Preferred: `Stable agent identity and role contracts are separate from presentation persona. Routing remains behind the Overseer and must preserve canonical authority, evidence and assurance semantics.`

Technical audiences may see role identifiers and provenance when implementation evidence supports them; persona is never a security principal.

## Default character language
- Willow — Planner persona.
- Isla — Executor persona.
- Jack — Guardian persona; communicates authority, permission, risk and boundaries.
- Henry — Assurer persona; communicates independent verification/PRS when canonical PRS evidence exists.

Never say `Jack approved it` when only UI state or a model recommendation exists. Never say `Henry verified it` without canonical PRS evidence.

## Content360 rule
Content360 may vary examples, tone, persona emphasis and audience framing. It may not:
- promote PRODUCT DIRECTION to PROVEN;
- invent implementation state for any of the 17 roles;
- imply direct specialist authority paths;
- imply every request uses all specialists;
- infer Green/PRS from persona language;
- turn a mascot or voice into an authority identity.

## Acceptance test
A campaign passes only if a reasonable reader can answer all four correctly:
1. Who do I interact with? — **The Overseer.**
2. Are specialist roles behind it? — **Yes, as the product model/direction.**
3. Does changing a name/face/voice change authority? — **No.**
4. Does `17 agents` mean 17 shipped autonomous employees doing every job? — **No.**

**OWNER-APPROVED PRODUCT DIRECTION + CLAIM-SAFE CAMPAIGN VOCABULARY.** Exact runtime implementation and availability remain evidence-gated.
