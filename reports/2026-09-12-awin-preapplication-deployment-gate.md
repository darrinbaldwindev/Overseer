# Awin Pre-Application Deployment Gate — 2026-09-12

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** PRE-APPLICATION READY / DEPLOYMENT-GATED

## Purpose

Convert the current Affiliate Websites architecture into an evidence checklist for Awin publisher onboarding. This gate prevents applying with placeholder domains or incomplete public surfaces.

## What is already present in the repository

The Affiliate Websites repo already contains:
- a global homepage and country-routing framework;
- an affiliate-disclosure pattern;
- footer navigation for Affiliate Disclosure, Privacy, Cookies & Tracking, Terms, Editorial Policy, Corrections, Methodology and Contact;
- a dedicated legal/trust page template;
- country separation for AU, UK and US;
- evidence/freshness principles and commercial-transparency copy;
- a publishability gate that requires publisher/account approval before a programme is called publishable.

These are strong structural signals, but repository presence is not proof that the equivalent pages are live on a production domain.

## P0 deployment evidence required before Awin application

### 1. Real publisher property
- at least one real public domain or subdomain is live;
- domain resolves reliably over HTTPS;
- site branding is stable enough for manual review;
- no placeholder `au.domain`, `uk.domain`, `us.domain` references are visible to users.

### 2. Public trust pages
The live site should expose working URLs for:
- Affiliate Disclosure;
- Privacy Policy;
- Cookies & Tracking;
- Terms;
- Editorial Policy;
- Corrections;
- Methodology;
- Contact.

Country-specific legal text must be reviewed before production publication. The existing master legal shell explicitly states that placeholder legal language is not final legal advice.

### 3. Representative editorial content
Before application, publish at least:
- 3–5 substantive editorial/research pages;
- at least one comparison page;
- at least one programme/platform profile;
- at least one methodology/trust explainer;
- content that shows sources or verification dates where material commercial facts are used.

The objective is to let a network reviewer see a genuine publishing property rather than an empty template.

### 4. Audience and traffic explanation
Prepare an accurate description of:
- AU/UK/US country scope;
- surveys/research/user testing/rewards/cashback categories;
- organic search/content strategy;
- social distribution through approved channels;
- Content360 as scheduling/distribution only;
- no spam, fake scarcity, deceptive claims or incentivised traffic unless a specific programme explicitly permits it.

Do not state traffic volume that does not yet exist.

### 5. Promotional-space register
Before onboarding, record each promotional property intended to be declared:
- primary website/domain;
- AU country destination;
- UK country destination;
- US country destination;
- approved Facebook/Instagram/TikTok/YouTube/X profiles if used;
- newsletters only if actually operating.

Do not later use undeclared traffic sources for programmes whose terms require disclosure/approval.

### 6. Publisher identity / commercial administration
Owner action will be required for:
- identity details;
- payout/tax/banking information where requested;
- terms acceptance;
- any verification payment/deposit;
- account credentials and MFA.

The Marketing Overseer must not invent or submit these details.

## Gate decision

### GREEN — apply
Only when:
1. live property exists;
2. trust pages work;
3. representative editorial content exists;
4. promotional spaces are known;
5. publisher description is accurate;
6. owner is ready to complete identity/payment/terms steps.

### AMBER — continue preparation
Current state. Repository architecture is strong, but live deployment evidence has not been verified here.

### RED — do not apply
If the proposed property is placeholder, empty, inaccessible, misleading about traffic, or missing required disclosure/contact/trust surfaces.

## After Awin publisher acceptance

Sequence:
1. verify network account and property acceptance;
2. inspect current programme terms in-account;
3. apply to strongest matching programmes;
4. store programme approval evidence;
5. create exact affiliate-resolution records;
6. test destination/attribution path;
7. only then mark monetised programme content PUBLISHABLE;
8. route approved social content through country-safe Content360 rules.

## Governance

No account creation, verification payment, terms acceptance, credentials, external application or live publishing is authorised by this report.
