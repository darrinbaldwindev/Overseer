# GhostKitchen + Franchise — Content360 Pre-Launch Plan

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** PRE-LAUNCH / EVIDENCE-GATED

## Purpose

Build audience and explain the operating model without making unvalidated earnings, ROI, legal, food-safety or franchise-success claims.

GhostKitchen is currently defined as a delivery-first food business/franchise platform with modular approved concepts and a shared operational/technology foundation. The Franchise platform is a home-based/local-fulfilment franchise system with a documented base franchise package and configurable business rules. Public marketing must remain subordinate to current business/legal evidence.

## Recommended Content360 structure

Begin with one umbrella pre-launch group if brand separation is not yet final:
- GhostKitchen / Franchise Build

Split into separate brand groups only when their customer/lead funnels are distinct.

## Content pillars

1. Delivery-first business education
2. Behind-the-scenes system building
3. Kitchen/menu/supplier/fulfilment operations
4. “How the model works” diagrams
5. Franchise-operator education
6. Technology and AI assistance in operations
7. Unit-economics concepts without income promises
8. Training/standardisation concepts
9. Local fulfilment/customer experience
10. Founder/build-in-public progress

## Safe social angles

- “What makes a delivery-first kitchen different from a traditional restaurant?”
- “Why standardise the operating system but keep concepts modular?”
- “How delivery cost changes the economics of free customer delivery.”
- “What a franchise dashboard should help an operator understand.”
- “Why actual hours and storefront availability are not the same thing.”
- “How supplier, menu and delivery systems fit together.”
- “Where AI can assist operations — and where professional/legal/food-safety judgement still matters.”

## Claims not to publish without stronger evidence/review

Do not claim:
- guaranteed earnings or payback period;
- typical income from a franchise;
- Centrelink/pension/tax outcomes;
- guaranteed profitability;
- guaranteed delivery economics;
- guaranteed customer demand;
- “turnkey success” or equivalent;
- legal/franchise compliance completion;
- food-safety compliance merely because processes are documented.

## Cadence

Pre-launch cadence should be lighter than an active ecommerce brand:
- 2 educational posts/week
- 1 build/progress post/week
- 1 operations explainer/week
- 1 audience question/poll/week
- occasional lead-capture content only after the offer and disclosure material are ready

## Funnel

Social education → project/interest landing page → information pack/waitlist → qualified enquiry → human review.

Do not route cold social traffic directly into a financial commitment flow until franchise/legal disclosure and sales processes are production-ready.

## Metrics

Track:
- qualified interest/waitlist;
- saves/shares on educational material;
- questions by topic;
- operator/franchisee lead quality;
- content themes that reduce repeated objections/confusion.
