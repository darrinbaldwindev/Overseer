# AgentOS Operator Tier Commercial Intent — 2026-09-12

**Status:** OWNER DIRECTION / MARKETING OVERSEER COMMERCIAL POSITION  
**Scope:** Product marketing and commercial positioning only. No runtime, scheduler, provider, credential, entitlement-enforcement, production activation, merge, approval, rebase or deployment changes.

## Owner direction

The current intended AgentOS commercial ladder is:

- **Free** — taste / prove usefulness
- **$39/year** — lower-commitment paid entry / fallback for users not yet ready for the recommended tier
- **$99/year** — **Operator; recommended/default destination and expected sale**
- Optional higher-capacity subscriptions, BYO AI, specialist capabilities, team/business offerings above this where appropriate

The $29/year tier is retired from the active commercial model except where preserved historically for comparison/testing records.

## Core commercial objective

The $99/year Operator tier should be positioned as the version that delivers the strongest practical benefit from AgentOS on a user's PC/laptop.

Marketing intent:

> **$99 Operator is the tier we want most users to choose.**

The $39 tier exists primarily as a lower-risk step for users who are not yet ready to commit to Operator.

## Value framing

At $99/year, Operator is approximately **$1.90 per week**, so public positioning may safely use:

> **Less than $2 a week.**

Recommended supporting positioning:

> **Put AgentOS to work for you for less than $2 a week.**

> **This is the version designed to give you the full practical benefit of AgentOS on your computer.**

> **It only needs to save you a small amount of time or AI spend each week to pay for itself.**

The stronger statement **“it will save that and more”** is the owner's commercial belief/intent but must not be used as an unconditional public savings guarantee until measured evidence supports it.

## Funnel role by tier

| Tier | Funnel role | Working message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Prove value / create habit | **Try AgentOS.** |
| $39/year | Lower-commitment entry / second chance | **AgentOS works with you.** |
| $99/year Operator | **Recommended tier / expected sale** | **Put AgentOS to work for you.** |

## Merchandising implication

Subject to implementation readiness and claim evidence, the $99 Operator tier should be the plan highlighted as **Recommended** across:

- pricing pages
- onboarding
- comparison tables
- upgrade prompts
- creator/reviewer briefs
- referral landing pages
- in-product value summaries and upgrade moments

$39 should not be visually or verbally positioned as the default destination if doing so weakens Operator conversion.

## Product packaging principle

$99 must feel materially better across multiple useful dimensions rather than being the same product with one arbitrary switch.

Candidate Operator advantages remain:

- broader bounded autonomy
- more schedules / Night Shift routines
- recurring/background work
- greater queue and concurrency capacity
- broader integrations
- larger AI/resource envelope
- stronger persistence/context capacity
- advanced checkpoint/recovery behavior
- richer monitoring/routing/control

$39 should still demonstrate enough of AgentOS's automation value to prove the benefit of upgrading; it should not become merely “$99 with a Run button.”

## Universal trust controls

The following should not be weakened or paywalled merely to force Operator conversion:

- pause / stop / kill controls
- permissions and approvals
- privacy/data controls
- Jack safety/authority enforcement
- Henry basic assurance/evidence visibility
- basic result/activity history where technically supported
- revocation and fail-closed behavior

## Marketing claim guardrails

Do not claim without evidence:

- guaranteed monetary savings
- guaranteed time savings
- that Operator always pays for itself
- that AgentOS always chooses the cheapest/best model
- that all Operator capabilities are implemented before runtime evidence exists

Preferred evidence-safe framing:

> **At less than $2 a week, AgentOS only needs to save a small amount of time or avoid a small amount of unnecessary AI spend to cover its cost.**

This is a value proposition, not a guaranteed savings claim.

## Current commercial hierarchy

**Free = taste**  
**$39 = “I’m not sure yet” / lower-commitment paid step**  
**$99 Operator = recommended/default destination and expected sale**

## Evidence classification

- **OWNER DIRECTION:** $99 should be the tier most users are encouraged to choose to obtain the greatest practical benefit from AgentOS.
- **FACT:** $99/year is approximately $1.90/week, therefore “less than $2/week” is valid arithmetic.
- **MARKETING POSITION:** Operator is the recommended/default paid destination.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Highlight Operator throughout the funnel while keeping $39 as the lower-commitment alternative.
- **CLAIM RESTRICTION:** Savings/payback language remains evidence-gated until measured user outcomes exist.
