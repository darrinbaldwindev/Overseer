## Commercial model under test

- **Free — Taste / Assistant:** deliberately limited but genuinely useful.
- **$29/year — Co-worker:** natural low-friction progression; more delegation, context, connections, AI resources and limited scheduled autonomy.
- **$99/year — Operator:** intended mainstream destination; materially greater scope, capacity, automation and autonomy.
- **Connected subscriptions / BYO AI:** additional capability for serious users subject to provider terms, policy, authority and budget.

## Key marketing principle

Sell outcomes, delegation and autonomy — not plugin/model counts.

The underlying free/premium/local provider pool remains strategically useful, but the user-facing question is:

> **How much useful work can AgentOS take off my hands?**

## Message variants to validate

### Resource-led
**You already have AI. AgentOS helps you make the most of it.**

### Delegation-led
**Tell AgentOS what you want done.**

### Autonomy-led
**Put AI to work.**

Decision must be based on comprehension, trust, activation intent and willingness to pay rather than internal preference.

## Night Shift validation

Current leading mainstream autonomy concept:

> **Give AgentOS work before bed. Wake up to the results.**

Validation must establish that users understand both the benefit and the safety boundary: time permission does not grant action authority.

## Evidence required before locking model

1. Novice comprehension test across message variants.
2. Real willingness-to-pay evidence for $29/year and $99/year.
3. Evidence Free is useful while retaining a natural upgrade reason.
4. Evidence $29 materially improves perceived capability/value.
5. Evidence $99 autonomy/scope is understandable and desirable.
6. Character-onboarding comprehension/trust test.
7. Night Shift comprehension/safety test.
8. Actual capability/cost modelling before promising included AI volume.

## Claims boundary

Do not claim guaranteed savings, optimal model selection, unlimited free AI, implemented autonomy/Night Shift before runtime evidence, cryptographic proof without implementation, enterprise-grade $99 capability without evidence, or current provider availability/limits without verification.

## Recommendation to CHATGPT Overseer

Preserve current commercial direction as a working hypothesis and route future implementation/UX work so it can support the above validation. Do not hard-code entitlement quantities, included AI volume or autonomy breadth until user and capability-economics evidence exists.
