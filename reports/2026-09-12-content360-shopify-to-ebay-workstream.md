# Content360 Workstream — Shopify → eBay

**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Status:** ACTIVE MARKETING WORKSTREAM
**Scope:** Portfolio marketing / Content360 distribution planning only. No production publishing, credential changes, marketplace listing writes, supplier contact, purchases or deployment.

## Decision

Add the **Shopify → eBay** project as a distinct Content360 marketing workstream rather than blending it into the main GlobalShopCo social feed.

This workstream remains commercially linked to GlobalShopCo but has a different audience, conversion path and proof burden:

- GlobalShopCo brand marketing = store/category/product discovery.
- Shopify → eBay marketing = marketplace-ready products, eBay-oriented discovery, seller trust, value, convenience and product-led demand generation.

Create a dedicated group:

**GlobalShopCo — eBay**

Recommended channel set where available and useful:

- Facebook
- Instagram

- YouTube Shorts

Do not assume every channel must receive every post. Content360 should repurpose a verified core asset into channel-native variants.

## Core content pillars

1. **Popular product discovery**
   - “Top finds this week”
   - category highlights
   - best-value / useful-product collections

2. **eBay-ready product spotlights**
   - one verified SKU/product per post
   - practical use case
   - quality / supplier credibility where evidenced
   - delivery / returns claims only where verified

3. **Category campaigns**
   - Home & Organisation
   - Consumer Electronics / Accessories
   - Pet
   - Baby / Family
   - Safety / Security
   - Tools / DIY
   - Automotive accessories
   - Health / personal-care accessories that are non-regulated
   - Garden / Outdoor
   - Lifestyle / convenience products

   Exact category list remains subordinate to eBay Overseer product research and actual viable inventory.

4. **Comparison / value content**
   - compare practical features
   - price/value context where current evidence exists
   - do not fabricate discounts, savings or competitor prices

5. **How-to / problem-solution content**
   - show the problem first
   - then the product as a useful solution
   - suitable for short-form video

6. **Trust content**
   - reputable supplier emphasis
   - shipping evidence
   - returns/warranty evidence
   - exact listing facts
   - avoid generic “best seller” / “top rated” claims unless evidenced

7. **Seasonal demand**
   - Christmas / gifting
   - summer / outdoor
   - back-to-school
   - home organisation
   - travel
   - weather-related utility

8. **Collections / bundles**
   - “5 useful products under …” only when current pricing is verified
   - “3 things for…”
   - room / activity / problem-based collections

## Product promotion gate

A product must not enter Content360 promotional rotation until the eBay/GlobalShopCo research record has enough evidence to support the public claim.

Minimum promotion-ready evidence:

- exact product / SKU identity
- supplier identity
- supplier reliability basis
- current source cost
- freight / delivery evidence
- landed-cost or margin assessment where relevant
- current eBay listing / market context where used in copy
- returns / warranty information where claimed
- stock / availability status where claimed
- image rights / usable source imagery
- explicit UNKNOWNs recorded

A candidate whose margin or free-delivery economics depend on missing freight evidence remains **BLOCKED FOR PROMOTION**.

## Content safety / accuracy rules

Do not publish unsupported claims such as:

- “cheapest on eBay”
- “best on eBay”
- “#1 seller”
- “free delivery” unless the actual listing supports it
- exact savings versus other sellers unless current evidence is captured
- stock urgency unless real inventory data supports it
- “Australian stock” unless verified
- branded authenticity claims without supplier/listing evidence

Use measured language:

- “worth a look”
- “useful pick”
- “one of the products we’re evaluating”
- “selected for quality/value review”
- “verified delivery terms at time of review”

## Recommended publishing mix

For each 7-day cycle:

- 2 product spotlight posts
- 1 category carousel / collection
- 1 short problem-solution video
- 1 educational / comparison post
- 1 trust / shipping / returns explainer
- 1 optional seasonal/trend post

Avoid seven consecutive direct-sales posts. Keep at least 30–40% of output useful/educational rather than purely promotional.

## Calls to action

Use varied CTAs:

- See the eBay listing
- View today’s pick
- Compare the options
- See the full collection
- Check current price and delivery
- See why we selected it

Do not imply a guaranteed current price in evergreen posts.

## Content360 architecture

Preferred flow:

**eBay Overseer / GlobalShopCo evidence → Marketing Overseer → approved core content → Content360 → channel-native variants → social channels → engagement/analytics → Marketing Overseer**

Content360 is a distribution/repurposing worker only. It must not become the source of truth for product data, price, stock, freight, margin or marketplace eligibility.

## Relationship to GlobalShopCo

The Shopify store remains the primary product/catalogue source where applicable. eBay is an additional marketplace/channel. Content360 should preserve the distinction between:

- GlobalShopCo website/store campaigns
- eBay-specific listing campaigns

Where the same product exists in both channels, copy should use the destination-specific CTA and current verified facts for that destination.
