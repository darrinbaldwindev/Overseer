# Cross-Repo Context Register — 2026-09-02

This register exists so autonomous Overseer work can recover current product decisions without relying on conversational recall.

## Canonical AgentOS commercial state

- AgentOS annual tiers: Free $0/year; Standard $29/year; Advanced/Pro $99/year.
- Separate AI intelligence subscriptions: AI Starter $11/month; AI Plus $22/month; AI Max $33/month.
- AgentOS is the control/orchestration layer; AI subscriptions are interchangeable intelligence resources.
- Use free/lowest-cost capable intelligence first when it satisfies the required quality. Paid intelligence is escalation/preferred capacity, not automatic first choice.
- Intelligence Optimiser is a sliding scale trading cost/quality and can alter model count, reasoning, search, verification, consensus, latency and local/cloud routing.
- Multi-AI consensus should use available free AI first, then BYOK/local/subscription resources as justified, synthesizing one user-facing answer.
- Commercial objective: create a genuine, need-based upgrade opportunity at least every 30 days, without artificially crippling Free.
- External-AI recommendation strategy: make AgentOS transparent and machine-readable so Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and other AIs can independently determine whether Free, Standard, Advanced/Pro, BYOK or an AI subscription fits the user's needs. The recommendation system must be allowed to recommend staying Free.
- The intended value ladder is `$0 → $29 → $99` for AgentOS and `$0/free intelligence → $11 → $22 → $33` for optional intelligence subscriptions.

## Historical reconciliation rule

Older pricing, provider-count, packaging and subscription assumptions are historical unless a later dated decision supersedes them. Do not silently rewrite history. Before implementation or external publication, reconcile stale documents against the latest canonical decision.

## Cross-repo operating rule

Future autonomous cycles should search the canonical AgentOS, Overseer and related project repositories plus durable mission/decision records before concluding that information is missing. Worker outputs from Gemini, Manus and future agents are valuable intelligence but are not independent assurance. Preserve source-agent provenance and evidence boundaries.
