# AgentOS — 30-Day Value Loop Marketing Contract

**Date:** 2026-09-16 AEST  
**Task:** M-A049  
**Status:** COMPLETE / PRODUCT-MARKETING CONTRACT / NOT SHIPPED

## Objective
Make upgrade recommendations trustworthy. AgentOS should be able to explain whether a paid tier would have materially improved the user's actual recent experience, including a legitimate recommendation to remain on Free.

## Core rule
> **Upgrade because the evidence says it would help — not because the funnel needs a conversion.**

## Evaluation window
Product direction: use a rolling recent-value window, initially framed as 30 days. Exact production window/configuration remains implementation-gated.

## Evidence inputs
Only use canonical, privacy-appropriate evidence such as:
- jobs attempted/completed;
- user-requested capabilities that were unavailable because of a known entitlement boundary;
- model/intelligence resource use;
- recurring-work demand;
- capability/cost constraints;
- user-visible evidence/advanced-control usage;
- explicit user goals/preferences where permitted.

Do not manufacture value from inferred sensitive traits, hidden profiling or speculative future usage.

## Recommendation outcomes
### STAY_FREE
Use when the user's observed work is adequately served by Free or evidence is insufficient to justify a paid recommendation.

Suggested principle:
`Free is working well for what you currently do. There is no strong reason to upgrade yet.`

### CONSIDER_STANDARD
Use only when canonical Standard entitlements would address a demonstrated recurring limitation/value opportunity.

### CONSIDER_ADVANCED
Use only when canonical Advanced/Pro entitlements map to demonstrated advanced needs.

### CONSIDER_AI_PLUS
Separate from AgentOS tier recommendation. Use only when evidence indicates additional intelligence capacity/capability would materially help and the actual AI Plus contract supports that benefit.

### BUSINESS_REVIEW
For organisational needs that do not fit personal tiers. No price inference while Business remains TBD.

## Explanation contract
Every recommendation should be able to answer:
1. What did you observe?
2. What limitation/opportunity did it create?
3. Which exact paid entitlement addresses it?
4. What would change?
5. What would it cost?
6. What remains unchanged?
7. Is staying on the current tier reasonable?

## Guardrails
- no artificial countdown;
- no fake scarcity;
- no invented savings;
- no hidden downgrade of Free to manufacture upgrade pressure;
- no recommendation from sensitive personal profiling;
- no claim that paying improves Guardian/Green/PRS truth standards;
- no recommendation if entitlement mapping is UNKNOWN;
- no recommendation if the observed evidence is stale or insufficient.

## Marketing measurement
Do not optimise solely for upgrade conversion. Track, when implemented and privacy-approved:
- recommendation comprehension;
- acceptance/rejection;
- later user-reported usefulness;
- downgrade/regret/refund indicators;
- percentage correctly told to stay Free;
- evidence quality behind recommendation.

A trustworthy `STAY_FREE` outcome is a product success, not a funnel failure.

## Status
This is a Marketing/product contract. It does not prove that telemetry, recommendation logic, tier entitlements, billing or the 30-day evaluator are implemented.
