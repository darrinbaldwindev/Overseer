## Invitation — default
We're preparing a small AgentOS Founding Beta for people who already use AI and have a real job they'd like AI to help complete.

AgentOS is being built around governed execution: you set the job and boundaries, AgentOS coordinates the work, and the product is designed to make authority, actions and evidence visible instead of simply giving AI unrestricted access.

For this first wave we're looking for a small number of testers willing to bring a genuine task, deliberately test the controls, and tell us where the experience is useful, confusing or untrustworthy.

This is an early controlled beta. Some work may stop rather than continue when authority or evidence is insufficient. We want that behavior tested as seriously as successful task completion.

If selected, you'll be asked to complete one real job, review what AgentOS says it did, test key controls where appropriate, and decide independently whether you would give it a second real job.

No payment, production-critical work or sensitive credentials should be assumed as part of the beta unless separately approved in the operating plan.

## Screening questions
1. Which best describes you?
   - Everyday AI user
   - Professional / small business
   - Creator / power user
   - Developer / technical user
2. How often do you currently use AI?
   - Rarely
   - Weekly
   - Most days
   - Several times a day
3. What real job would you bring to the beta?
4. Why would completing that job matter to you?
5. Which tools/files/services would the job need?
6. Does the job involve sensitive personal, customer, financial, medical, legal or credential data?
7. Could the job safely be attempted in a controlled non-production context?
8. Would you be willing to stop the task if AgentOS says it lacks authority or evidence?
9. Would you be willing to explain back what you think AgentOS did and what remains unverified?
10. After the first job, would you be willing to choose your own second job without being told what to try?

## Selection rubric
Prefer:
- genuine existing AI users;
- real bounded work, not demo theater;
- jobs with a clear expected outcome;
- jobs that can fail safely;
- mixed nondeveloper representation;
- testers willing to challenge evidence and controls;
- nearby/supportable Wave 0 participants where practical.

Avoid for Wave 0:
- production-critical irreversible work;
- high-stakes regulated decisions;
- tasks requiring unrestricted credentials or broad machine authority;
- participants primarily interested in stress-testing destructive behavior outside the defined fixture/scope;
- a developer-heavy cohort that distorts everyday usability evidence.

## Suggested 5–8 tester composition
- 2 Everyday users
- 1–2 professional/small-business users
- 1 creator/power user
- 1 developer
- optional 1–2 additional users from the strongest safe job candidates

## Expectation language
Tell every tester before starting:
- this is an early controlled beta;
- the product may deliberately refuse or stop;
- a refusal can be a successful governance result;
- apparent completion is not enough: evidence matters;
- they should not infer broader capability from one successful task;
- they should report confusing permission/evidence states immediately;
- they retain control over whether a task continues.

## Facilitator script boundaries
Facilitators may explain the interface and safety protocol. They must not:
- coach a tester into giving the desired trust score;
- choose the second job for the tester;
- reinterpret a failed control as success because the task outcome looked useful;
- hide support interventions;
- promote current technical claims beyond exact evidence.

## Real-job examples
Safe examples once the entry gate clears:
- inspect a bounded project and explain current state;
- modify a non-production document/config fixture inside an approved root;
- run an approved test/audit and explain the evidence;
- perform inspect → edit → test → verify on a disposable or controlled project;
- resume a bounded interrupted mission and show recovery state.

## Explicit non-promises
Do not say:
- AgentOS safely controls your whole computer autonomously;
- every result is independently verified;
- crashes can never cause inconsistent state;
- duplicate side effects are impossible;
- Stop instantly terminates every in-flight effect;
- credentials can never be exposed;
- the current beta is production-ready.
