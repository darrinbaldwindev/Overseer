## Purpose
Move supplier discovery away from candidates whose economics look interesting but whose marketplace rights are ambiguous. The first filter is now: **can the channel model itself be evidenced?**

## Current problem

## First permission-first lead — Dropshipzone / New Aim ecosystem
Current public retailer terms are materially stronger than ordinary generic dropship wording:
- the platform expressly enables retailers to buy, market and resell supplier products through their sales channels;
- retailers must comply with marketplace rules for any channel where products are listed;
- retailers are responsible for creating/maintaining their own marketplace listings unless specifically authorised otherwise;
- supplier-specific terms can still apply;
- Dropshipzone provides product information intended for resale through retailer sales channels.

Dropshipzone also publishes eBay-specific guidance acknowledging that compliant eBay fulfilment requires the seller to pre-purchase or otherwise own inventory, be identified as the seller on packing slips/invoices, meet stated fulfilment commitments, and restrict third-party use of eBay customer data.

### Marketing interpretation
This is a **better permission foundation for research**, not proof that any specific SKU is eBay-ready.

It materially reduces one ambiguity: the retailer model is explicitly designed around resale through sales channels. But exact eBay eligibility still requires SKU/supplier/fulfilment/account checks.

## Dropshipzone/New Aim evidence gate before any SKU can advance
For each candidate, require:
1. exact supplier and SKU/EAN;
2. product/category not restricted by platform or eBay policy;
3. explicit confirmation that the chosen fulfilment/ownership model satisfies eBay's current requirements;
4. GlobalShopCo seller identity preserved on buyer-facing packing/invoice material;
5. buyer data used only for fulfilment as required;
6. Australian stock location evidenced when listed as Australian stock;
7. exact trade/acquisition cost including GST treatment;
8. exact freight/landed cost for representative AU destinations;
9. live stock/feed behavior;
10. returns/warranty terms;
11. achievable retail/eBay comparison set;
12. positive free-delivery contribution after fees and operating reserve;
13. actual GlobalShopCo eBay fee/plan evidence;
14. owner publication authority.

## Candidate-shape filter
Search this ecosystem first for:
- compact preferably sub-1kg products;
- non-fragile, low-complexity items;
- Home Organisation / pet / safety-adjacent products with clear dimensions;
- AOV high enough that parcel freight does not dominate contribution;
- low variant/compatibility risk;
- clear product identity/EAN;
- supplier terms/returns that can be represented honestly.

Avoid initially:
- bulky furniture;
- low-AOV single items with high parcel freight;
- products with contradictory titles/specifications;
- high-return fit/compatibility products;
- regulated/safety-critical products without complete compliance evidence;
- rows depending on placeholder or retail—not trade—cost.
