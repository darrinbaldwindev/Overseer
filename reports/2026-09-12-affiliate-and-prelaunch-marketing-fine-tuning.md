**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** CURRENT DIRECTION / EVIDENCE-GATED

## Affiliate Websites

**Primary promise:** Find reputable ways to earn rewards without wasting time on poor offers.

The public brand should optimise for trust and usefulness, not affiliate-program breadth.

### Message hierarchy
1. Is this reputable?
2. Is it available in my country?
3. What does the user actually get?
4. How much effort/friction is involved?
5. What are the important restrictions?
6. Only then: compare options.

### Recommended page structure
Hero: **Find reward programmes worth your time.**
Supporting copy: Compare reputable survey, research, testing and reward opportunities available in your country.
CTA: **Compare verified programmes**

### Ranking principle
Do not rank primarily by affiliate payout. Weight user reward, reputation, current country availability, friction, payout threshold, evidence freshness and conversion quality.

### Content ratio
For every 10 posts:
- 3 verified programme spotlights
- 2 comparison/roundup posts
- 2 educational/scam-avoidance posts
- 1 higher-value research/focus-group opportunity explainer
- 1 transparency/disclosure post
- 1 community/question post

### Country differentiation
AU: lead with reputable local survey/research/testing availability and clarity.
UK: emphasise trust and non-financial reward/research opportunities first; keep regulated financial topics separate.
US: broaden into research participation, testing and higher-value opportunities, while clearly distinguishing referral programmes from B2B affiliate programmes.

### Conversion refinement
Social → country article/comparison → programme detail → outbound tracked link.
Avoid sending cold social traffic straight to affiliate links except where the user already has enough context.

## GhostKitchen

**Primary promise:** A delivery-first operating system for modern food businesses.

Fine-tune pre-launch content around operational intelligence, not franchise hype.

Best themes:
- why delivery-first economics differ;
- how menu, suppliers and delivery fit together;
- standardise systems, customise concepts;
- operator dashboards and performance visibility;
- what AI should and should not automate.

CTA: **Follow the build** / **Register interest** only when the lead funnel is ready.

Avoid “low-cost business”, “passive income”, “turnkey success” and projected earnings claims without validated evidence.

## Franchise

**Primary promise:** A structured local-fulfilment business system.

The strongest eventual differentiation is likely the system: territory, catalogue controls, contribution tracking, earned-hours logic, operator support and central technology. Market the system before the opportunity.

Recommended sequence:
1. Explain how the model works.
2. Explain costs/contribution honestly.
3. Explain support/technology.
4. Explain who it may suit.
5. Then present enquiry/lead capture.

Do not use Centrelink, pension, tax or employment-benefit implications as acquisition copy.

## GemVerse

Remain discovery-gated. Marketing may refine brand/canon consistency internally, but public acquisition should wait for an authoritative current product, audience, launch scope and monetisation snapshot. Historical/archive material must not be turned into current product promises.

## MyPrimeDelivery

Remain discovery-gated. Do not build campaign positioning from the name. Resume only when the customer, problem, operating model, geography, offer and first usable workflow are authoritative.

## Shared principle

Pre-launch marketing should reduce uncertainty and build qualified interest. It should not compensate for unresolved product/commercial definition with excitement language.
