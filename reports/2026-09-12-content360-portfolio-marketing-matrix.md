## Shared Content360 architecture

Project evidence/source of truth
→ Marketing Overseer
→ claims/product gate
→ approved content package
→ Content360
→ social channels / RSS / link-in-bio
→ engagement + analytics
→ Marketing Overseer
→ project learning backlog

Content360 is a replaceable distribution worker. It does not own canonical product facts, pricing, availability, claims, customer data, campaign strategy or approval authority.

## Universal publication gates

Before publication, content must have:
- current source evidence for material factual claims;
- current price/availability/delivery evidence where relevant;
- image/asset use rights;
- project-appropriate legal/compliance review where relevant;
- no guaranteed savings, earnings, ROI, safety, performance or availability unless verified;
- correct country targeting for affiliate/commercial programmes;
- a traceable source record or campaign ID.

## Portfolio cadence principle

Content volume should follow evidence and customer value, not a quota. Each active stream should maintain at least three content types: useful/educational, proof/product, and conversion. Reposting must adapt copy and creative to the channel rather than duplicate identical text everywhere.
