- authenticated/current supplier cost, stock and marketplace/resale permission;
- dropship/packing compatibility where relevant;
- actual freight basis;
- current exact-product eBay comps;

- conservative contribution calculation;
- freshness and contradiction checks.

- Baby/Home Safety product promotion without product-specific compliance evidence.

## Evidence-expiry rules

For future SKU promotion:
- price, stock, current listing and shipping/freight are Class A fast-changing facts;
- recheck immediately before publication and before reposts outside the allowed freshness window;
- supplier permission and exact identity must remain bound to the same SKU/variant;
- stale evidence moves the asset to `REVERIFY-EVIDENCE` and removes the direct CTA.

## Conversion graduation checklist

Content360 may only transform a candidate into a direct product spotlight when the Marketing/project handoff contains:
1. exact supplier SKU/variant;
2. exact live eBay listing identity;
3. current supplier buy cost;
4. current stock semantics;
5. marketplace/resale permission;
6. freight basis for the target Australian market;
7. actual eBay fee/category basis;
8. conservative delivered contribution calculation;
9. current returns/warranty facts if used;
10. image/asset rights;
11. freshness timestamps and contradiction check;
12. owner-authorised publication state.

Missing any required commercial value keeps the conversion asset blocked.

## Performance interpretation

Category engagement may create a research priority but cannot promote a SKU to `EBAY-ELIGIBLE`.

Track where available:
- saves/shares by category;
- video completion;
- comments requesting specific products/problems;
- category-page clicks;
- audience questions about price/shipping;
- trust-content engagement.

Feed these signals back to Marketing/eBay research as demand evidence only. Supplier, freight, fee, permission and listing evidence remain authoritative for commercial eligibility.
