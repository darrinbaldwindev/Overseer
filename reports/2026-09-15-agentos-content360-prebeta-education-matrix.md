Safe educational foundation:
- AgentOS is being built around governed AI execution rather than unrestricted computer access.
- The product direction is one Overseer relationship coordinating specialist roles.
- Current development deliberately separates authority, execution evidence, readiness and independent assurance.
- Current bounded Windows/PowerShell work is under active development and remains evidence-gated.
- Provider/model neutrality and coordinated use of free/local/paid AI are product direction/architecture themes; exact availability remains implementation-dependent.

- Level 2 complete;
- Founding Beta open;
- safe autonomous control of the whole computer;
- every result independently verified;
- physical Windows mutation acceptance;
- durable revoke everywhere;
- voice available;
- 17 shipped autonomous employees.

## Content families
### 1. `Why governed execution matters`
Explain the difference between answering and acting. Emphasise authority, evidence and failure states. No readiness claim.

### 2. `One Overseer, specialist roles behind it`
Explain the user relationship and persona/role separation. Do not imply all 17 roles are shipped.

### 3. `What happened?`
Teach why evidence should be visible after AI action. Use `bounded What happened evidence summary` language, not complete Evidence Timeline unless implementation later clears that gate.

### 4. `Green is not Henry`
Explain conceptually that execution success, Green verification and PRS/assurance are distinct. Avoid implying current completion-grade PRS PASS.

### 5. `Ready for what?`
Teach readiness separation: chat availability, host capability, physical acceptance and mutation readiness are different questions.

### 6. `MCP compatibility is not a trust certificate`
Explain capability provenance, permissions and evidence as a future trust contract. Do not disparage competitors or claim uniqueness without evidence.
