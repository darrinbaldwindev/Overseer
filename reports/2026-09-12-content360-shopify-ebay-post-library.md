# Shopify → eBay — Content360 Post Library

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** ACTIVE / EVIDENCE-GATED

## Goal

Create a reusable library for the ten established eBay categories while preserving a strict distinction between category education and product-specific promotion.

## 1. Home Storage & Organisation
**Hook:** The fastest way to improve a room is often to remove friction, not add furniture.
**Copy:** Think drawer dividers, shelf organisers, stackable storage, cable control and under-used vertical space. We shortlist products that solve one clear organisation problem well.

## 2. Pet Accessories
**Hook:** Useful pet products earn their place through everyday convenience.
**Copy:** Travel, feeding, cleanup, storage and comfort are stronger categories than novelty alone. Product posts require current listing and supplier evidence.

## 3. Kids Storage & Organisation
**Hook:** Better storage makes cleanup easier for everyone.
**Copy:** Look for simple systems that help toys, school items, books and everyday gear return to the same place.

## 4. Kitchen & Pantry Organisation
**Hook:** A pantry should help you find things, not hide them.
**Copy:** Good products improve visibility, access and use of shelf depth. Category-level advice can run before exact products are cleared.

## 5. Mobile & Computer Accessories
**Hook:** The best desk accessories usually solve a tiny repeated annoyance.
**Copy:** Cable routing, charging, stands, adapters and storage all work when compatibility and build quality are clear.

## 6. Garden & Outdoor Utility
**Hook:** Outdoor products should solve practical jobs first.
**Copy:** Storage, watering, carrying, organising and maintenance are useful starting points. Seasonal demand never replaces evidence checks.

## 7. Baby & Home Safety
**Hook:** Safety claims need more than a supplier headline.
**Copy:** For safety-related products, we need current specifications and stronger evidence before direct promotion. Until then, content stays educational.

## 8. Automotive Accessories
**Hook:** Car organisation is mostly about reducing small daily annoyances.
**Copy:** Storage, charging, cleaning, securing loose items and practical utility are better angles than novelty.

## 9. Fitness & Exercise Accessories
**Hook:** Small fitness accessories should support the habit, not promise the outcome.
**Copy:** Storage, comfort, setup and simple training support are valid. Avoid body-transformation or performance guarantees.

## 10. Cleaning & Household Utility
**Hook:** If a product saves one annoying step, it may be worth a look.
**Copy:** The strongest household utility items improve reach, storage, cleanup, organisation or repeatability.

## Product spotlight template

**Hook:** One useful product. One clear job.

**Copy template:** We shortlisted [PRODUCT] because it addresses [PROBLEM]. Current listing evidence confirms [ONLY VERIFIED FACTS]. Check the current listing for live price, availability and delivery details.

**CTA:** View the current listing.

## Comparison template

**Hook:** Which option fits the job best?

Compare only verified dimensions such as size, compatibility, materials, capacity, delivery basis, return terms and current price. Do not fabricate a winner if the evidence is incomplete.

## Trust post

**Hook:** Why some products never make the feed.

**Copy:** Missing freight evidence. Unclear supplier identity. Weak returns terms. Questionable safety claims. Stale pricing. If the basics are uncertain, promotion waits.

## Behind-the-scenes post

**Hook:** We research the problem before promoting the product.

**Copy:** The eBay stream starts with categories people actually use, then narrows into individual products only when the commercial and listing evidence is strong enough.

## Short-video pattern

1. Show the everyday problem in 2–3 seconds.
2. Show the product/category solution.
3. Give one verified fact only.
4. Show current-listing CTA.
5. End: “Useful products. Carefully checked.”

## Publishing boundary

Category-level content may run with no exact product. Direct product CTAs require live listing evidence. Any stale price, freight, availability or returns information automatically downgrades the content back to category education until reverified.
