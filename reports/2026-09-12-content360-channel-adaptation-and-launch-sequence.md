# Content360 Channel Adaptation + Initial Launch Sequence

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** EXECUTION-READY / EVIDENCE-GATED

## Channel adaptation rules

### Facebook
Use the longest explanatory copy. Lead with the problem, then the useful takeaway, then CTA. Best for trust, comparisons, founder/build posts and product/category explanations.

### Instagram
Use stronger visual hierarchy, concise captions and carousels. Lead with a clear hook in the first card/frame. Best for before/after, team/character content, category shortlists and trust carousels.

### TikTok
Use 15–30 second problem-first videos. First two seconds must show the problem/question. Avoid generic brand intros. Best for demonstrations, “would this actually help?”, “why we rejected this”, and quick explainer formats.

### YouTube Shorts
Use a slightly more complete narrative than TikTok: hook → context → proof/process → CTA. Strong for AgentOS explanations, product vetting, category education and affiliate opportunity types.

### X
Use short claims, observations, threads and evidence updates. Avoid long promotional copy. Strong for AgentOS product thinking, benchmark updates, product-research decisions and new verified programme additions.

### LinkedIn
AgentOS and build/operations content only unless a project has a clear professional audience. Use professional outcomes, governance, systems and lessons. Low priority for consumer ecommerce/product posts.

## Reuse rule

One core idea may be reused across channels, but copy and creative framing must be adapted. Content360 should distribute variants, not identical cross-posts.

## Promotion escalation

A stream may move from educational to direct-response only when its evidence gate is cleared:
- AgentOS: benchmark/runtime proof for stronger capability/savings claims.
- GlobalShopCo: exact product + freight + price + delivery facts.
- Shopify→eBay: exact live listing + current delivery/returns facts.
- Affiliate Websites: current country/programme/reward/referral evidence.

## Content360 workflow status

1. Draft creative package in canonical report/content source.
2. Validate claims/evidence.
3. Adapt per channel.
4. Approve asset + copy.
5. Schedule/publish via Content360.
6. Capture publication URL/receipt where available.
7. Feed engagement/traffic observations back into the project learning backlog.

Content360 does not become the canonical campaign database; publication receipts and performance summaries should be copied back into portfolio records when operational automation is implemented.
