# GlobalShopCo — Content360 Marketing Plan

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** ACTIVE / PRODUCT-EVIDENCE-GATED

## Role

GlobalShopCo is the primary ecommerce brand and Shopify source. Content360 should distribute approved product, category, educational and seasonal content without becoming a source of truth for price, stock, delivery or supplier claims.

The Shopify→eBay workstream is related but separate. GlobalShopCo social should promote the broader store/customer proposition; the eBay stream should focus on eBay-suitable product/category discovery and eBay conversion.

## Core content pillars

1. Useful product discovery
2. Home organisation and everyday utility
3. Baby / pet / safety category education where product evidence supports it
4. Product comparisons
5. Problem-solution demonstrations
6. Free-delivery value messaging only where the current offer actually supports it
7. Trust: delivery, returns, warranty and product-selection standards
8. Seasonal/use-case collections
9. “Why we shortlisted / rejected this product” transparency
10. Helpful guides from headless/WordPress content

## Product promotion gate

Before a named SKU/product is promoted, verify the material facts used in the creative:
- supplier/listing identity;
- current product availability;
- wholesale/landed-cost assumptions where relevant to internal viability;
- freight evidence;
- customer delivery proposition;
- current retail price;
- returns/warranty facts used in copy;
- product specifications/safety claims;
- image/asset rights.

If freight evidence is missing and margin depends on it, the product remains blocked from promotional conversion content.

## Weekly Content360 cadence

- Monday: category/use-case education
- Tuesday: evidence-cleared product spotlight
- Wednesday: short problem-solution video
- Thursday: useful guide / headless article repurpose
- Friday: comparison or shortlist
- Saturday: seasonal/lifestyle use case
- Sunday: weekly finds / trust/behind-the-scenes post

## Content package per strong product

Create:
- 1 hero image;
- 1 3–5-card carousel;
- 1 15–30 second vertical video;
- 1 Facebook/Instagram long caption;
- 1 short social caption;
- 1 landing/category blurb;
- 1 link-in-bio entry;
- evidence/source record;
- expiry/recheck date for dynamic claims.

## Main stream vs eBay stream

### GlobalShopCo main
CTA: browse GlobalShopCo/store/category content.
Primary story: useful products, carefully selected, straightforward shopping.

### Shopify→eBay
CTA: verified eBay listing/category where appropriate.
Primary story: selected practical products available through the eBay channel.

Do not duplicate the same post to both feeds unchanged. Re-angle by channel and destination.

## Headless role

GlobalShopCo-Headless/WordPress should support deeper editorial guides, comparisons and SEO/AEO content. Approved articles can feed Content360 through RSS where suitable, preserving Shopify as the product/checkout source of truth.

## Metrics

Track by category/SKU/creative:
- CTR;
- product/category views;
- add-to-cart where available;
- purchases/attribution;
- saves/shares;
- video completion;
- returns/customer questions;
- margin viability changes;
- evidence freshness.

A high-engagement product should still be removed from promotion if current freight, price, stock or supplier evidence no longer supports the offer.
