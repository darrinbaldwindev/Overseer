# AgentOS Pricing, Upgrade Journey and Member Referral Loop — 2026-09-07

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Scope:** AgentOS commercial positioning, onboarding, upgrade conversion and referral strategy  
**Status:** RECOMMENDATION / HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION REQUIRED

## Decision context

The current AgentOS commercial ladder is being refined from **Free → $29/year → $99/year → subscriptions/BYO AI**. New user direction proposes testing **$39/year** in place of $29/year to improve the commercial bridge to $99 while increasing retained annual revenue from customers who never upgrade.

This record does not approve final pricing. It captures the current marketing recommendation and the validation questions that must be answered before price, entitlement, onboarding cadence or referral economics are treated as settled.

## 1. $39/year as a new bridge-price candidate

### Working proposal

Test the following ladder:

- **FREE — Taste:** deliberately limited but genuinely useful AgentOS experience.
- **$39/year — Co-worker:** natural low-friction paid progression with materially more AI capability, delegation, memory/context, integrations and limited scheduled autonomy.
- **$99/year — Operator:** intended mainstream destination with substantially greater autonomy, capacity, recurring work, Night Shift/Autonomy Hours, broader integrations and more advanced recovery/assurance.
- **Additional subscriptions / BYO AI:** for serious users needing extra providers, specialist capability, high usage, business features or connected external subscriptions.

### Economics framing

- $39/year is approximately **$0.75/week**.
- $99/year is approximately **$1.90/week**.
- The upgrade difference is **$60/year**, approximately **$1.15/week**.

### Marketing rationale

**HYPOTHESIS:** $39 may create a stronger commercial bridge than $29 because:

1. it remains below $1/week and therefore retains a low-friction value story;
2. it increases annual revenue from customers who remain at the middle tier indefinitely;
3. it narrows the perceived gap to $99;
4. it may make $99 feel like a more natural step-up rather than a 3.4x jump from $29;
5. it gives more room to include genuinely useful Co-worker capability without making the middle tier economically weak.

### Validation requirement

Test **$29 vs $39** before finalising the bridge tier. Measure actual willingness to pay and later upgrade behaviour rather than relying only on stated preference or simulated checkout intent.

## 2. Commercial role definitions

### FREE

**Taste AgentOS.**

Free should prove the product but remain deliberately limited. The user should experience useful AI, natural-language delegation and the AgentOS team without receiving the breadth, capacity, persistence or autonomy intended for paid plans.

### $39/year — Co-worker

**Get much more from AgentOS for less than $1/week.**

Working entitlement themes:

- expanded free-AI capability pool;
- more and better AI resources;
- more complex and multi-step work;
- greater memory/context;
- more integrations;
- higher usage/capacity;
- repeatable workflows;
- limited scheduled delegation / limited Night Shift exposure.

### $99/year — Operator

**Let AgentOS take more work off your hands.**

Working entitlement themes:

- significantly greater autonomy;
- multiple Autonomy Hours / Night Shift routines;
- larger queues and more recurring work;
- broader Context Space scheduling;
- more concurrent/parallel work;
- higher AI/resource capacity;
- broader integrations;
- stronger recovery/checkpoint/resume;
- more advanced budget/routing controls;
- more serious work/business capability.

### Subscriptions / connected AI

For serious users needing specialist providers, external subscriptions, additional premium AI, larger usage, business/team features or advanced integrations.

## 3. First-week Willow / Isla / Jack / Henry introduction

The earlier concept of introducing one character per calendar week should be replaced with a **first-week, activity-triggered introduction**.

All four should normally be introduced during the first week, but each introduction should occur when the relevant role is naturally demonstrated.

### Willow

**Role:** understands and plans.

Trigger examples:
- first meaningful job;
- first multi-step plan;
- first task decomposition.

### Isla

**Role:** executes.

Trigger examples:
- first action or multi-step execution;
- first browser/file/tool job;
- first generated deliverable requiring active work.

### Jack

**Role:** controls authority, permissions and risk.

Trigger examples:
- first approval boundary;
- first connected-service action;
- first budget or potentially irreversible decision.

### Henry

**Role:** checks, verifies and assures.

Trigger examples:
- first completed result requiring evidence/verification;
- first source-backed research output;
- first Night Shift/Morning Brief result.

### Governing rule

The characters are **AgentOS functions**, not separate products or feature buckets. Marketing must not reposition Willow as a briefing tool, Isla as only research, Jack as a drafting feature or Henry as Night Shift. Night Shift uses the whole team.

## 4. 30-day upgrade nurture

The first 30 days remain a **commercial nurture period**, but not a date-gated feature unlock.

### Week 1

Introduce all four characters through real product use where practical.

### Days 8–30

Use contextual, periodic reminders when the user's behaviour exposes a meaningful paid benefit.

Examples:

- Willow: "This could become a repeatable workflow on Co-worker."
- Isla: "Operator could run this while you're away."
- Jack: "Paid plans give you more control over scheduled work and connected tools."
- Henry: "Operator can assure larger and more complex autonomous jobs."

These are conceptual examples, not final user-facing copy.

### Anti-nagging rule

Do not show repetitive generic upgrade banners. Upgrade prompts should be tied to actual value, actual limits or a task the user has attempted.

## 5. Post-Day-30 weekly upgrade reminder

After Day 30, a Free user may receive an upgrade reminder approximately every **7 days**, subject to product-level notification controls and anti-spam safeguards.

The reminder should be **value-based and personalised**, not a repeated generic sales popup.

Potential examples:

- "You've completed 14 AgentOS jobs. Co-worker gives you more capacity and workflows."
- "You've manually run this task three times. Operator could schedule it."
- "You've reached your Free AI allowance. Upgrade to give AgentOS access to more capability."
- "Several of your recent jobs could have used Night Shift."

### Guardrail

Frequency alone does not justify contact. The final implementation should include suppression rules for inactive users, dismissed prompts, recent upgrade decline, notification preferences and user annoyance signals.

## 6. Member referral programme

AgentOS should distinguish two acquisition systems:

### Creator / Affiliate Programme

For influencers, publishers, reviewers, AI media and strategic partners. Existing working recommendation remains a separate creator-affiliate model with verified attribution/economics to be validated before launch.

### Member Referral Programme

For ordinary AgentOS users who genuinely find the product useful and want to refer friends, colleagues or family.

Working message:

> **Invite someone who would find AgentOS useful.**

Possible rewards should be tested and need not be cash. Options include:

- extra AI allowance;
- extra Night Shift runs;
- temporary Operator capability;
- account credit;
- bonus premium capability;
- limited-time specialist AI access.

No reward design is approved yet.

## 7. Desired word-of-mouth position

The desired organic user reaction is that AgentOS is useful and makes accessing/using AI easier.

A strong natural referral explanation would be:

> **"Instead of figuring out which AI to use, I just give AgentOS the job."**

Potential official positioning remains more evidence-gated:

- **Put AI to work.**
- **AgentOS brings your AI together and helps you make the most of it.**
- **A simpler way to access and use AI.**
- **One platform. Every AI.**

AgentOS should not claim to be **"the best way to access AI models"** without substantiated comparative evidence. Users may independently express that opinion, but official marketing claims require evidence.

## 8. Referral timing

Do not ask for referrals immediately after signup.

Trigger referral prompts after demonstrated value, for example:

- multiple completed jobs;
- repeat active use;
- positive explicit feedback;
- successful Night Shift/Morning Brief;
- meaningful saved workflow;
- a user milestone such as 20–25 completed jobs.

This creates a value-first referral loop rather than an acquisition-first interruption.

## 9. Relationship to unified AI / BYOK / repository research

The user-provided Unified AI & Repository Management Guide records existing demand patterns around unified AI access, BYOK, local models, repository-aware workflows and routing across tools such as Cursor, Aider and Cline. The strategic AgentOS opportunity is not to copy those developer products, but to generalise the value for non-developers and broader work:

> **The user does not manage a model dropdown; the user gives AgentOS the job.**

This remains a strategic interpretation, not proof of market demand.

## 10. Marketing validation priorities

Highest-value next tests:

1. **$29 vs $39 bridge-price test** — real willingness to pay and later upgrade intent.
2. **$39 vs $99 comprehension test** — can users explain the difference in plain language?
3. **Limited Night Shift at middle tier vs expanded Night Shift at $99** — does experiencing limited autonomy increase $99 desire?
4. **First-week character introduction** — do Willow, Isla, Jack and Henry improve understanding/trust without causing terminology overload?
5. **30-day contextual nurture** — does value-triggered education outperform calendar-only messaging?
6. **Weekly post-Day-30 reminder** — measure conversion lift versus annoyance/unsubscribe/suppression signals.
7. **Member referral reward test** — determine whether AI allowance, Night Shift runs, account credit or temporary premium access produces the highest qualified referral rate.
8. **Word-of-mouth test** — ask users how they naturally describe AgentOS to another person without supplying marketing language.

- **RECOMMENDATION:** After Day 30, test a value-based weekly upgrade reminder with suppression/notification controls.
