# AgentOS Marketing Funnel Fine-Tuning — 2026-09-12

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** CURRENT DIRECTION / CLAIM-GATED

## Core positioning

**Primary promise:** Put AI to work.

**Plain-language explanation:** AgentOS helps you give AI a real job, then coordinates the AI available to you—free, local and paid—within your rules, limits and permissions.

**Primary CTA:** Give AgentOS a real job.

## Message hierarchy

### Hero
**Put AI to work.**
Stop juggling AI tools. Give AgentOS the job and let it coordinate the work.

CTA: **Give AgentOS a real job**
Secondary CTA: **See how AgentOS works**

### Proof strip
Use four compact proof themes rather than feature lists:
- Works with the AI available to you.
- Designed to make free and local AI more useful.
- Built around permissions, limits and verification.
- One AgentOS, from simple everyday use to advanced control.

### Team story
Introduce Willow, Isla, Jack and Henry after the user understands the product promise. They explain how AgentOS works; they are not the headline proposition.

### Free AI story
Lead with savings of complexity before savings of money:
“You may already have enough AI to get useful work done. AgentOS is designed to coordinate what you already have before reaching for more.”

Do not claim free-only effectiveness as measured fact until the benchmark clears its gate.

### Pricing story
Free: Try AgentOS and see the workflow.
$39 Co-worker: AgentOS works with you.
$99 Operator: AgentOS can work for you, on your schedule.

Operator should be visually recommended.
Supporting line: **$99/year — less than $2 a week.**

Do not make Co-worker look deliberately crippled. It should be a legitimate paid option; Operator wins by stronger capacity, persistence, recurring work and autonomy envelope.

## Funnel simplification

Ad / social → one real-job example → landing page → safe first task → demonstrated result → upgrade prompt → later referral prompt.

Avoid sending cold traffic directly into complex architecture, agent-character detail, provider matrices or governance terminology.

## Best first-job categories for marketing

Prioritise examples understandable in under five seconds:
- “Summarise these files and tell me what matters.”
- “Research this and give me the best options.”
- “Turn these notes into a plan.”
- “Check this project and tell me what needs attention.”
- “Prepare this work for tomorrow.” (only where actual scheduling/autonomy evidence supports the exact example)

## Operator conversion logic

Operator should be sold on three combined benefits:
1. **Less AI juggling** — AgentOS coordinates models/providers.
2. **More work completed** — longer-running, recurring and higher-capacity workflows.
3. **More useful time back** — benchmark this rather than promise it categorically.

The conversion question should be:
**“Would having AgentOS handle more of this for you be worth less than $2 a week?”**

## Social content ratio

For every 10 AgentOS posts:
- 3 real-job/problem-solution examples
- 2 free/local AI value posts
- 2 trust/governance posts in plain English
- 1 team/mascot post
- 1 Operator value post
- 1 proof/benchmark/build update

This prevents the feed becoming either pure price promotion or pure technical explanation.

## Creator strategy refinement

Ask creators to test AgentOS on a real job from their own workflow, not recite a feature list. Best content format:
- what I normally do;
- what I gave AgentOS;
- what happened;
- what still required me;
- whether I would use it again.

## Claim boundaries

Until independently evidenced, do not say:
- “AgentOS always picks the best AI”;
- “AgentOS will save more than $99/year”;
- “AgentOS replaces your AI subscriptions”;
- “AgentOS works with every AI”;
- broad “works while you sleep” claims without the precise implemented/runtime scope.

## Main test

A/B the hero:
A: **Put AI to work.**
B: **Stop choosing AI. Start giving it jobs.**

Keep the same CTA and downstream page so the test isolates message resonance.
