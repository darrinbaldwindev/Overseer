## Shared rules

- Always name the country in programme-specific posts.
- Do not imply that a consumer/member referral programme is a publisher affiliate programme.
- Do not state typical earnings unless independently supported.
- Programme reward/commission terms must be reverified before publication if outside the freshness window.
- Prefer routing traffic to the relevant Affiliate Website country page, where current disclosures and comparison context can be shown.
