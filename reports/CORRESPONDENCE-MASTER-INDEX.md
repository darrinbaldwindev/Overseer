## Reconciliation rules

1. Material correspondence must be entered here.
2. Mission records must link material correspondence identifiers.
3. Worker/provider reports must preserve source attribution.
4. Cross-repo handoffs must identify source repository and destination/reconciled repository state.
5. Duplicate or superseded correspondence is retained and marked accordingly.
6. No correspondence entry is execution evidence by itself.
