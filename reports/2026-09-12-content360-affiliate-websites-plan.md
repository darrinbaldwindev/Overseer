# Affiliate Websites — Content360 Distribution Plan

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer  
**Status:** ACTIVE / COUNTRY-SEPARATED / EVIDENCE-GATED

## Scope

Applies to the Affiliate Websites master architecture plus AU, UK and US country implementations. Content360 is a distribution layer only; country workstreams remain authoritative for local programme facts, eligibility, rewards, referral terms and compliance.

## Account groups

Create separate groups:
- Affiliate Websites AU
- Affiliate Websites UK
- Affiliate Websites US

Do not publish one country's programme terms into another country's feed unless the programme is verified as available there.

## Primary content pillars

1. Reputable rewards/survey/referral programme discovery
2. “How it works” education
3. Reward-type explainers: cash, gift card, points, testing, focus groups, research participation
4. Programme comparisons
5. Scam/red-flag education
6. Country-specific “best current options” roundups
7. New verified programme additions
8. Editorial articles generated from the WordPress country sites
9. User-value stories only when evidence exists
10. Disclosure/transparency content explaining affiliate relationships

## Preferred automation path

WordPress approved article → RSS → Content360 campaign → country social feeds → traffic back to article.

This is the preferred first automation because RSS is an established Content360 feature and avoids assuming an undocumented developer API.

## Weekly cadence per country

- Monday: verified programme spotlight
- Tuesday: educational tip / scam avoidance
- Wednesday: comparison or category roundup
- Thursday: article/RSS repurpose
- Friday: higher-value opportunity type (focus group/testing/etc.) where locally verified
- Saturday: community question / “what have you tried?”
- Sunday: weekly verified roundup / new additions

## Publication gates

Every named programme post must have current evidence for:
- country availability;
- user reward type;
- referral/affiliate status if described;
- eligibility/material restrictions;
- material payout/threshold statements;
- current landing URL;
- disclosure language;
- last-verified date.

Do not publish exact earnings claims as typical outcomes unless independently supported. Prefer ranges only when the source establishes them and label variability clearly.

## Conversion design

Social content should normally drive to the country website rather than directly to an affiliate link. The site remains the explanatory/verification layer and owns disclosures, comparison context and outbound affiliate resolution.

## Country emphasis

### Australia
Lead with reputable survey/research programmes, cashback/reward discovery, user testing and focus groups that genuinely reward Australian users.

### United Kingdom
Prioritise non-financial rewards/research initially; keep FCA/ASA-sensitive financial material separately reviewed. Watch voucher/code abuse and attribution issues.

### United States
Broader paid-participation ecosystem: surveys, focus groups, B2B research, user testing, product testing and other locally available reward programmes, with clear distinction between consumer referral and B2B affiliate models.

## Metrics

Track:
- article CTR;
- outbound programme CTR;
- signup/conversion where attributable;
- saves/shares;
- search/referral traffic lift;
- programme freshness/expiry;
- country-specific conversion quality.

Do not optimise solely for the highest affiliate payout; user reward, reputation, fit and evidence quality remain part of ranking.
