## Asset F1 — How we judge a rewards programme

**Format:** Carousel  
**Cards:**
1. Does it actually reward the user?
2. Is it available in this country?
3. Is the programme reputable enough to recommend?
4. Are the payout/threshold terms clear?
5. Is there a legitimate referral/affiliate path?

**Caption:**
A high affiliate payout does not automatically make a programme worth recommending.

We rank user reward, reputation, country fit, clarity and evidence alongside commercial value.

## Asset F3 — Scam/red-flag checklist

**Format:** 5-card carousel  
**Cards:**
- unclear company identity;
- unrealistic earnings promise;
- payment required to access ‘jobs’;
- vague reward/withdrawal terms;
- no credible support/privacy information.

**Caption:**
If an earning opportunity sounds effortless, guaranteed or unusually high, check the details before signing up.

**CTA:** Read the safer-opportunity guide.

## Creative direction

- Trustworthy editorial/research look, not “make money fast” styling.
- No cash-rain, luxury lifestyle or guaranteed-income imagery.
- Country identity should be obvious in AU/UK/US assets.
- Exact earning claims require current source support and clear variability context.
- Social normally links to the country website first, not directly to affiliate links.
