## Scope
Apply the canonical relationship/freshness gate to Australian paid-survey/referral programmes. This is a consumer-value/editorial shortlist, not proof of publisher-affiliate permission.

## Relationship taxonomy
A programme may be:
- consumer reward only;
- consumer referral only;
- publisher affiliate only;
- dual relationship;
- unknown.

Do not infer publisher monetisation rights from an ordinary member referral programme.

## Current shortlist conclusion
For an Australian consumer-facing `Surveys that pay` page, Octopus Group and Pureprofile both pass the **current consumer reward + member referral evidence** screen.

They do **not** yet pass the separate **publisher affiliate** gate from the evidence reviewed here. Therefore:
- editorial recommendation can proceed when pages preserve current terms and freshness dates;
- ordinary member referral monetisation should not be assumed appropriate for a publisher website;
- any publisher-level CTA/commission attribution remains HOLD until explicit programme permission/terms are evidenced.

## Freshness rule
Referral/reward amounts, qualifying activity, caps, country availability and redemption terms are volatile. Reverify within 90 days before promotional ranking or monetised CTA use, and immediately if the provider changes its terms.

## Ranking dimensions for the next AU batch
Evaluate additional programmes separately on:
1. current user reward evidence;
2. current consumer referral evidence;
3. separate publisher affiliate evidence;
4. Australian availability;
5. payout/redemption clarity;
6. eligibility/caps;
7. reputation/compliance evidence;
8. data-feed/linking practicality;
9. terms freshness;
10. suitability for editorial vs monetised CTA.

## Hard rule
**Consumer referral is not publisher affiliate permission.** A programme may be excellent for users while still being unsuitable for publisher monetisation.
