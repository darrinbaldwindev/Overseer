# Affiliate Websites — Programme Relationship & Freshness Proof Gate

Date: 2026-09-14 Brisbane
Owner: Marketing Overseer
Status: CANONICAL MARKETING/EDITORIAL GATE

## Purpose
Prevent the AU/UK/US affiliate sites from confusing publisher affiliate relationships with consumer referral/reward programmes or promoting stale terms.

## Relationship taxonomy
### Publisher affiliate
The site earns commission for sending a user to a merchant/programme. This does **not** imply the user receives a reward.

### Consumer referral
A user/referrer may receive a reward for referring another user. This does **not** automatically create a publisher affiliate relationship for the website.

### User-reward programme
The user earns cash, points, gift cards or other benefit through participation/purchase/activity. This does **not** prove there is an affiliate/referral programme available to the publisher.

### Dual relationship
Both a meaningful user reward and an evidenced publisher/referral commercial path exist. This is the preferred class where the proposition promises the user is rewarded.

## Minimum evidence before promotion
For every programme/country row capture:
- programme name + canonical URL;
- relationship type(s);
- country eligibility;
- user reward mechanism and material conditions;
- publisher/referral commercial route and network/direct source;
- current signup/availability state;
- payout/reward evidence date;
- important exclusions/minimums/eligibility constraints;
- last verified date + evidence source.

## Freshness classes
- **CURRENT**: primary evidence checked within 30 days for volatile terms.
- **RECENT**: 31–90 days; editorial mention allowed with recheck before CTA/publication.
- **STALE**: >90 days for volatile commercial/reward terms; no promotional CTA until reverified.
- **UNKNOWN**: no reliable current evidence; research/editorial hold.

## Publication states
- PASS — exact country + relationship + reward/commercial evidence current.
- EDITORIAL ONLY — programme can be discussed neutrally but CTA/commercial claim held.
- HOLD — relationship/reward eligibility incomplete or stale.
- REJECT — misleading fit, unavailable country, or proposition conflicts with site promise.

## Claim rules
Never write `earn money`, `get rewarded`, `cashback`, `refer and earn`, `available in Australia/UK/USA`, or payout amounts unless current evidence supports that exact programme/country/relationship.

Never infer publisher affiliate availability merely because a consumer referral link exists. Never infer consumer rewards merely because an affiliate network lists the merchant.

## Country application
AU, UK and US workstreams may maintain independent programme evidence, but the master template owns this taxonomy and freshness/status semantics so country sites remain comparable.

## Marketing recommendation
Prioritise dual-relationship programmes and strong user-reward programmes with an independently evidenced commercial route. Awin/CJ/Impact/network membership is discovery evidence, not proof that every programme is active, available or user-rewarding in every country.

## Hard boundary
No monetised CTA or programme ranking from stale/unknown evidence. Fresh evidence outranks prior lists and research notes.
