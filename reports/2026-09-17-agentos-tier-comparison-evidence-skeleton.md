## Hard commercial rule
Safety truth must not become a luxury feature. A lower tier must not imply lower evidence honesty, weaker permission truth or a right to bypass governance.

Paid tiers may legitimately offer more capacity, convenience, advanced controls, capabilities or intelligence where the canonical entitlement model eventually says so. Marketing must not invent that allocation first and force Product to match it later.
