## Portfolio rule

Marketing should answer these questions in order:
1. What does this help me do?
2. Why should I believe it?
3. What should I do next?
4. What does it cost / what is the commitment?
5. How does it work in more detail?

Do not reverse this order by leading with implementation detail.

## Message compression rule

Each asset should communicate one dominant idea. Secondary benefits belong below the fold, later in the carousel, or in follow-up content. Avoid combining price, features, governance, architecture, characters and competitor positioning into one ad.

## Proof rule

The stronger the promise, the stronger the proof required. Broad category education may publish with source-backed category evidence. Named product, programme, earnings, savings, time, performance, safety, price, delivery and availability claims require current item-level evidence.

## Channel role

- Short video: problem → contrast → proof → CTA.
- Carousel: explain or compare.
- Static image: one memorable promise.
- Long-form article: evidence and depth.
- Landing page: conversion hierarchy.
- Email/nurture: objections, proof and activation.
- Content360: distribution and repurposing only; not strategy or source of truth.
