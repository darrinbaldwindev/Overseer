## Claim discipline

`member referral` is not `publisher affiliate`.

The country site may explain member referral rewards only from current primary evidence. It must not imply that the site owner earns a publisher commission unless a separate publisher contract, network listing or official publisher terms are current and evidenced.

Volatile reward amounts, caps and redemption terms remain freshness-gated and should be rechecked before publication.

## Next AU research target

Continue with programmes that can independently prove all three where possible:
1. Australia availability;
2. genuine user reward;
3. current referral and/or publisher relationship.

Prioritise dual-relationship programmes because they better align user value with sustainable site economics, but do not downgrade reputable non-monetised comparison alternatives merely because publisher revenue is absent.
